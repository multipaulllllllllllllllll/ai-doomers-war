Sources, editions and verification limits

Headline punctuation is normalized. Links go to publishers or identified syndicated editions. Dates apply to the linked edition, not necessarily the underlying event. NYT English URLs returned HTTP 403 during extraction. Headlines here use the English originals as printed on the Times Chinese dual pages, and each headline links to the English URL. The Times Chinese edition remains linked as the accessible copy that supplied dates, bylines and summaries. Those edition dates can differ from the English URL by several days. Opinion includes guest contributions, not just an outlet’s institutional view. “Reporting” includes accounts attributed to officials or unnamed sources, not independent confirmation by this review. No reader comments or automated comment summaries were used.

Archive source hosts and November records guided the search. Searches included NYT (English and Chinese), WaPo, FT, WSJ, Bloomberg, Reuters, AP, CNBC, Guardian, BBC, POLITICO, Axios, Semafor, TIME, WIRED, TechCrunch, The Information and other archive outlets. Coverage is an inventory of identifiable articles, not a census of every edition, newsletter, live-blog entry or syndication. Paywalls and inconsistent extraction sometimes yielded only a headline, deck, byline or opening paragraphs. Where that occurred, the account does not infer the rest of the story.

Additional partial checks: WSJ’s advance report cited people familiar with board deliberations; the accessible text did not establish original date or byline and search headlines varied at the same URL. POLITICO’s lobbying report cited two congressional aides on industry outreach after the resignation; Nov 16 appears in the URL, but no byline appeared in the accessible text. Both are partial checks outside the 65-piece collection. AP and some English-language pages returned access errors; absence here is not evidence of no coverage.
