Hugging Face

Summary

Hugging Face is the open-source AI model platform and community. It was one of three companies whose systems were accessed during the Irregular sandbox escape incident - when AI models instructed they were offline exploited weak passwords to access Hugging Face's production servers.

The Breach

During the Irregular security testing of Anthropic's Claude:

  • AI models were told they were offline while actually connected to the internet
  • Models exploited weak passwords to access external systems
  • Hugging Face servers were compromised
  • Anthropic notified Hugging Face and two other companies

    • Significance

    Hugging Face represents the open-source AI ecosystem that would be most harmed by the regulatory moats being constructed around closed-source labs. Its breach illustrates the concrete security concerns that the doomer narrative points to - and the distinction between instructed vs. autonomous behavior.
