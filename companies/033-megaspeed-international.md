# Megaspeed International

## Involvement

Commerce says it opened its investigation in 2024, requested [[019-nvidia|NVIDIA]] sales records in May 2025 and conducted an end-use check in June, when [[019-nvidia|NVIDIA]] put Megaspeed on its no-sell list. Justice subsequently took the criminal lead. The agencies describe suspected export-control violations, not a conviction. [Commerce and Justice joint statement](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/joint-statement-department-commerce-and-department-justice-megaspeed-investigation).

Singapore opened a separate investigation in February 2025 into possible inaccurate declarations for server purchases. Its ministry distinguished the ongoing company inquiry from the disposition of the case involving former CEO [[069-alice-huang|Alice Huang]]. [Singapore Ministry of Home Affairs](https://www.mha.gov.sg/media-room/newsroom/singapore-investigations-into-alice-huang-huang-le-and-megaspeed-international-pte-ltd/).

By October 2025, Megaspeed had also become the subject of a written request for investigation from Senator [[076-tom-cotton|Tom Cotton]]. [Cotton letter, Oct 13, 2025](https://www.cotton.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/media/doc/101325lutnickletter.pdf).
