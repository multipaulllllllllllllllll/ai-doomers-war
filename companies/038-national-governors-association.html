National Governors Association

Association publishing state and federal AI governance recommendations

Involvement

The association published principles developed by its bipartisan Governors Council to Secure American AI Dominance. The governors asked Congress to develop a national AI framework while preserving an appropriate state role. These are recommendations to lawmakers, not a federal law or a binding agreement among states. National Governors Association.
