Megaspeed International

Singapore company investigated over server purchases and possible chip diversion

Involvement

Commerce says it opened its investigation in 2024, requested NVIDIA sales records in May 2025 and conducted an end-use check in June, when NVIDIA put Megaspeed on its no-sell list. Justice subsequently took the criminal lead. The agencies describe suspected export-control violations, not a conviction. Commerce and Justice joint statement.

Singapore opened a separate investigation in February 2025 into possible inaccurate declarations for server purchases. Its ministry distinguished the ongoing company inquiry from the disposition of the case involving former CEO Alice Huang. Singapore Ministry of Home Affairs.

By October 2025, Megaspeed had also become the subject of a written request for investigation from Senator Tom Cotton. Cotton letter, Oct 13, 2025.
