Holy See (Vatican)

Summary

The Vatican entered the AI doomer war in May–October 2026 as something none of the existing players are: a global moral authority with no incentive structure the labs can impugn. The Holy See's positions, from an actual encyclical plus a papal flight presser, map precisely onto the wiki's fault lines: power concentration (vs. the Nvidia/$2T-IPO economics), autonomous weapons ("practically beyond any human reach to govern"), algorithmic redlining, data colonialism, and a direct call for government regulation of AI companies. In September it became the war's loudest counter to Trump's "hoax" framing.

Institutional Moves

  • May 15/25, 2026, Magnifica Humanitas. First AI encyclical; signed on Rerum Novarum's 135th anniversary, deliberately equating the AI transition with the industrial revolution. First pope personally presenting an encyclical. Co-presenter: Anthropic's Christopher Olah (see 056-christopher-olah).
  • Vatican AI Commission: promotes dialogue/study/investigation "to find ways to make sure that AI does not get to a point of destroying humanity or removing humanity from the conversation"; has fielded inbound interest from tech figures since the encyclical, per Leo on the plane (Sept 28).
  • Fr. Brendan McGuire (Silicon Valley parish, Vatican-tech liaison): "Tech industry leaders see something in what they're developing that is concerning them, maybe even frightening them... it would be morally reprehensible for us to not partner with them."
  • Claude Constitution contributors include Bishop Paul Tighe (Dicastery for Culture & Education), a formal channel already opened between the lab and the Curia.
