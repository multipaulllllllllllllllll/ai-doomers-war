# National Governors Association

## Involvement

The association published principles developed by its bipartisan Governors Council to Secure American AI Dominance. The governors asked Congress to develop a national AI framework while preserving an appropriate state role. These are recommendations to lawmakers, not a federal law or a binding agreement among states. [National Governors Association](https://www.nga.org/news/press-releases/governors-call-for-ai-action/).
