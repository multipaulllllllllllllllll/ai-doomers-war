# Polymarket

## Summary

Polymarket is the largest crypto-based prediction market — a venue where users take positions on real-world outcomes (elections, wars, Fed rates, and yes: AGI timelines and AI-caused catastrophes) priced in USDC. It matters in this wiki on two axes: **(1)** it is a fossil of the SBF/EA money web — Alameda Research's roughly $40M undisclosed early stake, revealed by WSJ during FTX's collapse — and **(2)** it is now the institutionalized face of "doom as a tradeable": after the crypto wilderness years, NYSE-parent ICE invested ~$2B (October 2025) and the platform re-entered US regulated territory.

## The Hidden-Money Precedent (the EA angle)

When FTX fell (November 2022), WSJ reported that **Alameda Research had quietly invested in Polymarket** — a reported ~$40M of a $70M round (Lightspeed and Pantera announced later; Alameda's share wasn't disclosed at the time). Alongside **Vitalik Buterin's** angel stake and **Founders Fund's** 2020 seed lead, it made Polymarket a map of the crypto-EA-VC overlap zone of 2020–2021:

- The same era that produced "earning to give"
- The same circles where frontier-lab seed money and prediction-market bankroll were indistinguishable bets on the future
- The same pattern this wiki keeps documenting: **money that funded the network's infrastructure didn't announce itself**

In September 2026 X discourse, that history resurfaces every time doom content goes viral: reply-guys under Polymarket's AI markets cite SBF as proof that "the network" gambles on what it preaches.

## AI Doom as Market

The frontier-AI question set is now literally tradeable: AGI-by-date contracts, first-major-incident markets, model-release and IPO timing spreads. The wiki's recurring cast shows up as counterparties:

- **Doom buyers** hedge what the labs' own essays predict (Amodei's "pace" as a catalyst contract)
- **Accelerationist sellers** monetize the same essays' backlash
- Critics of EA ("tech scientology" frame) allege the network funds both the product (fear) and the exchange (markets) that prices it

Whether that's a coherent conspiracy or just a market is, fittingly, an open position.
