# Good Ventures / Coefficient Giving

## Summary

Good Ventures (now evolving into "Coefficient Giving") is the philanthropic vehicle of Dustin Moskovitz and Cari Tuna. It is the largest private funding stream directed at AI safety organizations in the Effective Altruism ecosystem. Its documented FY2025 grants include $78.8M to Founders Pledge, $14.4M to FAR AI, and $11.5M to Effective Ventures USA.

## The Funding Network

Coefficient Giving / Good Ventures represents the financial pipeline that connects tech wealth to the AI safety ecosystem. Its grants fund:
- The organizations that produce safety research (MIRI, Epoch AI)
- The policy organizations that translate safety research into regulation (ControlAI)
- The evaluator that gates frontier AI deployments (METR)
