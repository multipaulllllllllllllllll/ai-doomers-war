# Senate Banking Committee

## Involvement

The committee scheduled a December 3 open hearing on U.S. export controls and named [[019-nvidia|NVIDIA]] president and CEO [[068-jensen-huang|Jensen Huang]] as its witness. [Senate Banking hearing notice](https://www.banking.senate.gov/hearings/protecting-americas-ai-advantage-oversight-of-us-export-controls).

On November 12, committee member [[077-jim-banks|Jim Banks]] had publicly sought testimony from both Huang and Commerce Secretary [[060-howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]]. A member’s request is distinct from the committee’s witness notice. [Banks interview transcript, Nov 12](https://www.banks.senate.gov/news/press-releases/icymi-sen-banks-joins-america-reports-on-fox-news-channel-4/).
