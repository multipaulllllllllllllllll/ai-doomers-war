Future of Life Institute

Summary

The Future of Life Institute (FLI) is the organization that coordinated the March 2023 "Pause Giant AI Experiments" letter, which called for a 6-month moratorium on AI training runs beyond GPT-4 level. The letter was signed by Elon Musk, Steve Wozniak, and multiple Turing Award winners, and provided significant political cover for subsequent regulatory arguments.

The Pause Letter

The March 2023 letter stated:

"Contemporary AI systems are now becoming human-competitive at general tasks, and we must ask ourselves: Should we let machines flood our information channels with propaganda and untruth? Should we automate away all the jobs, including the fulfilling ones? Should we develop nonhuman minds that might eventually outnumber, outsmart, obsolete and replace us?"

The letter called for a 6-month pause on training runs beyond GPT-4 level.

Funding

FLI was early-funded by Dustin Moskovitz, the Facebook co-founder who is also the lead investor in Anthropic's Series A. This connects FLI's regulatory advocacy to the same financial network that benefits from the regulations FLI advocates for.

Significance

FLI represents the media/campaign dimension of the doomer ecosystem - translating technical arguments into public pressure campaigns that provide political cover for regulation.
