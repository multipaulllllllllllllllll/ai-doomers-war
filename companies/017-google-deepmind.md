# Google DeepMind

## Summary

Google DeepMind is the AI research division of Alphabet Inc., created through the merger of Google's AI teams with the London-based DeepMind acquired in 2014. It is the creator of the Gemini model family and Google's primary response to the frontier AI race. DeepMind has historically had a stronger safety research culture than its competitors, but is now under commercial pressure to accelerate deployment.

## Position in the Debate

DeepMind represents the established tech giant in the AI race. It is less associated with the doomer narrative than Anthropic, but its parent company's interests align with the regulatory framing that would advantage incumbents over scrappy startups.

## Key Products

- **Gemini**: Family of frontier AI models
- **AlphaFold**: Protein folding research (Nobel-adjacent)
- **AlphaCode**: Coding AI
