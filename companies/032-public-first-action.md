# Public First Action

## Summary

Public First Action is a bipartisan 501(c)(4) social welfare organization. [[Anthropic]]'s newsroom (Feb. 12, 2026) called it "newly formed" and announced a $20 million donation. A second newsroom note (Jul. 21, 2026) announced another $20 million, "bring[ing] our total support for Public First Action to $40 million." Both notes say the gifts "cannot be used to influence the election of any candidate for federal, state, or local office" and are restricted to public education and policy work. The stated site is publicfirstaction.us.

This is not [[national-philanthropic-trust]]. NPT is a 501(c)(3) public charity. Public First Action is a 501(c)(4).

## What [[Anthropic]] says the money is for

The February 12, 2026 note lists three policy priorities: model transparency rules; "the need for a federal AI framework that does not preempt state laws"; and "smart export controls on AI chips." The July 21, 2026 note repeats the restriction against candidate elections and says both gifts support "Public First Action's public education and policy mission."

## What Axios says

Axios (Jul. 22, 2026) described Public First Action as a 501(c)(4) "tied to three super PACs that back candidates who support AI safeguards," and quoted [[Anthropic]]'s restriction language. This sheet records that framing as Axios's. It does not treat the $40 million as candidate contributions. IRS Form 990s and FEC super-PAC links were not pulled.

## Significance

The gifts sit on the same export-controls lane as [[Jack Clark]]'s later public-benefit work at [[Anthropic]]. They are a company-funded 501(c)(4), not a lab-safety evaluator and not a political committee on the company's own description. See [[anthropic]].
