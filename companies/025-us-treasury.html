U.S. Treasury (Bessent)

The financial state enters the AI argument: via sanctions, suspicious-activity reporting, and a liability doctrine that denies the labs their favorite excuse.

Two tools, one message

  1. Liability, not immunity. Bessent on CNBC: "It is humans who are responsible": OpenAI management owns the Hugging Face attack; no shield for a "rogue AI" defense. The labs' capability narrative, inverted: if the model really is dangerous, someone is negligent.
  2. Sanctions, not safety gates. The HF/Irregular breakouts recast as "state-backed hacks using open-source AI": Sept 22 sanctions (2 Russian entities, 6 individuals), SARs to banks, FinCEN advisories, AI-CSAC/AI-FOCUS, G7 agenda. The gate proposed is a national-security border, not an evaluation regime.

Why it matters to the graph

  • Every actor in this wiki who funded evaluations (Coefficient/NPT → METR/Irregular) or demanded a gate (FLI, CSER, the pacing coalition) now faces a Treasury that routes through money, not metrics
  • The "paid activists" jab at EA's Capitol Hill expansion (Sept 16) is the first senior-official echo of the wiki's core funding-loop claim
  • A gate is still a gate: "open-source AI is a national security risk" serves the same moat function as "unsafe frontier models": the political side just swaps who gets excluded

November 5-13, 2026: Megaspeed listing dispute

Axios reported on November 11 that Bessent pressed Howard Lutnick at the November 5 Situation Room meeting on why Megaspeed was not on the Entity List. Sources portrayed Bessent, Rubio, Greer and Hegseth as favoring controls, with Musk supporting them when Trump called him. Lutnick reportedly resisted. Commerce denied that Bessent and Lutnick had the reported confrontation.

The Megaspeed memo was reportedly drafted by Rubio and handed to Lutnick, not authored by Commerce. An unnamed source disparaged Lutnick's response to Bessent, not Bessent the memo. No liar quotation or Trump probable-override quotation is substantiated by these Axios articles.

CBS separately reported Bessent's November 13 CNBC remarks that Treasury, War, USTR and State supported listing for months but "Commerce has the pen." This public statement is not independent confirmation of the private meeting. The chip-export dispute is distinct from Treasury's September sanctions, SARs and FinCEN channel.

Sources: Axios Situation Room; Axios Oval Office; CBS, public remarks and testimony.
