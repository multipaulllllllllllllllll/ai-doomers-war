China Hawks

Faction label for officials opposing NVIDIA chip sales and PRC open-weight models in U.S. systems; not a legal entity

Summary

"China Hawks" in this wiki is a faction label for the November 2026 cluster of U.S. officials who tried to keep NVIDIA chips out of China, publish the H200 license mix, and pull PRC open-weight models out of federal systems. It is not a registered organization, not a White House task force, and not the Super Intelligence Force. Readers should not treat the slug as a membership roll that anyone signed.

Who is on the page, and who is not

Hawk nodes this sweep:

  • marco-rubio, Secretary of State and National Security Adviser, reportedly drafted the Megaspeed memo (Axios, November 11, 2026)
  • scott-bessent, Treasury Secretary, reportedly pressed for Megaspeed restrictions (Axios, November 11, 2026)
  • brian-mast, House Foreign Affairs, H200 license release
  • andrew-garbarino, House Homeland Security, Nov. 20 open-weight report
  • john-moolenaar, House Select Committee on the CCP, same report

Reported resistance: Axios sources portray Howard Lutnick as opposing or delaying Megaspeed restrictions. Commerce denied the reported Bessent-Lutnick confrontation. The memo was reportedly drafted by Rubio and handed to Lutnick; an unnamed source disparaged Lutnick's reply, not Bessent the memo.

Principal: Axios reported Trump approved action on November 5 after consulting Musk, who supported controls. The reports do not substantiate a presidential probable-override quotation. NVIDIA's November 23 reinstatement of Huang did not conclude the independent investigation or government inquiries.

Sources: [Axios, November 10](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/10/lutnick-nvidia-jensen-huang-china-memo); [Axios, Situation Room, November 11](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/11/bessent-lutnick-megaspeed-nvidia-china-chips); [Axios, Oval Office, November 11](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/11/trump-lutnick-nvidia-china-chip-export-controls); [NVIDIA, November 23](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director).

Two products, one week

  1. Chips. Rubio's reported memo and the committee's November 19 disclosure of BIS's May 11 summary: 3,444,160 NVIDIA H200 and AMD MI325X chips requested, 879,868 approved across 21 applications. These are combined product totals, not shipments. Maginfra and Beijing New Matrix are among potential installers, not necessarily purchasers. Sources: [HFAC summary, November 19](https://democrats-foreignaffairs.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/11.19.26-ECRA-Information-Disclosure-HFAC-Summary.pdf); [BIS table as of May 11](https://democrats-foreignaffairs.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/11.19.26-ECRA-Information-Disclosure-of-BIS-License-Decisions.pdf).
  2. Weights. Garbarino-Moolenaar November 20 report: PRC open-weight models in federal systems and high-risk infrastructure; four findings; fourteen recommendations whose titles are not invented here.

The November 19 BIS remote-access rule and the TSMC 16-nanometer halt sit under us-commerce and tsmc, not as a third hawk "product," even though they move in the hawk direction.

What to Watch

Whether the reported presidential decision becomes a documented Entity List action, and whether the House open-weight recommendations lead to legislation. Approval counts, shipments and investigation outcomes remain separate questions.
