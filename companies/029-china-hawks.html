China Hawks

Faction label for officials opposing NVIDIA chip sales and PRC open-weight models in U.S. systems; not a legal entity

Summary

"China Hawks" in this wiki is a faction label for the November 2026 cluster of U.S. officials who tried to keep NVIDIA chips out of China, publish the H200 license mix, and pull PRC open-weight models out of federal systems. It is not a registered organization, not a White House task force, and not the Super Intelligence Force. Readers should not treat the slug as a membership roll that anyone signed.

Who is on the page, and who is not

Hawk nodes this sweep:

  • marco-rubio, Secretary of State (New York Times, Nov. 12, 2026)
  • howard-lutnick, Secretary of Commerce, Megaspeed memo (Axios, Nov. 11, 2026)
  • brian-mast, House Foreign Affairs, H200 license release
  • andrew-garbarino, House Homeland Security, Nov. 20 open-weight report
  • john-moolenaar, House Select Committee on the CCP, same report

    • Counterweight, not a hawk: scott-bessent told the Cabinet Lutnick's memo was "bullshit" and that Lutnick had a "long track record of being a liar" (Axios, Nov. 11, 2026; AP). Bessent has his own character sheet and the Treasury org page. He is linked here so the fight is one graph, not two.

    Principal: donald-trump said he would "probably" overrule Lutnick (Axios, Nov. 11, 2026), called Huang a "brilliant man" (Los Angeles Times), and later told Reuters he had referred Huang's situation in a talk with Xi.

    Two products, one week

  • Chips. Lutnick memo plus Mast's BIS May 11 figures (about 3.44 million H200s requested, 879,868 approved, 75,000 Alibaba, 150,000 Tencent, Maginfra and Beijing New Matrix named).
  • Weights. Garbarino-Moolenaar November 20 report: PRC open-weight models in federal systems and high-risk infrastructure; four findings; fourteen recommendations whose titles are not invented here.

    • The November 19 BIS remote-access rule and the TSMC 16-nanometer halt sit under us-commerce and tsmc, not as a third hawk "product," even though they move in the hawk direction.
