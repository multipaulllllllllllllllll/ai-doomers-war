# OpenAI

## Summary

OpenAI is the AI lab founded in 2015 by Sam Altman, Ilya Sutskever, Greg Brockman, and Wojciech Zaremba. Creator of the GPT series and ChatGPT, OpenAI has evolved from a nonprofit research lab to a $150B+ commercial enterprise. In September 2026, OpenAI co-signed the three-lab pacing agreement with Anthropic, ruled out a 2026 IPO, and had its CEO (Sam Altman) in contact with Barack Obama about AI policy.

## The Pacing Agreement

OpenAI co-signed the three-lab agreement within approximately 48 hours of Jacob Coxon's exit letter. This was notable because OpenAI and Anthropic are primary competitors. The coordination required explanation — the most compelling being that both labs benefit from the same regulatory environment that the doomer narrative creates.

## The IPO Ruling Out

CFO Brad Lightcap told investors OpenAI is at least 18 months from an IPO, explicitly linking the delay to "safety work not being ready." This parallels Anthropic's framing: both labs are using safety as the reason for not going public, even as they pursue maximum commercial value.

## Internal Divisions

OpenAI has had documented internal divisions:
- **Mira Murati**: Former CTO who reportedly pushed for a 6-month pause before resigning
- **Wojciech Zaremba**: Co-founder who has expressed concern about internal politics overriding safety
- **Ilya Sutskever**: Former Chief Scientist who left to found SSI, previously led the Superalignment team
