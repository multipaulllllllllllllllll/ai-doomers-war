# Conjecture

## Summary

Conjecture was an AI governance organization founded by Connor Leahy and Gabriel Alfour. It served as the organizational predecessor to ControlAI: Andrea Miotti was Head of Strategy & Governance at Conjecture before founding ControlAI, and Leahy and Alfour moved from Conjecture to run ControlAI operations.

## The Alumni Pipeline

Conjecture trained the individuals who became the policy infrastructure of the doomer regulatory movement:

- **Andrea Miotti**: Head of Strategy & Governance → Founder of ControlAI
- **Connor Leahy**: Co-founder → Heads ControlAI US
- **Gabriel Alfour**: Co-founder → Helps run ControlAI
