xAI

Summary

xAI is Elon Musk's AI company, founded in 2023 to compete with Anthropic and OpenAI. The company released the Grok family of models commercially. Musk's position on xAI is characteristically contradictory: he has called AI more dangerous than nuclear weapons, signed the FLI pause letter, and positioned himself as a doomer, while simultaneously building xAI as fast as possible.

The Mixed Position

xAI represents the contradiction at the heart of the anti-doomer camp: Musk simultaneously calls AI existentially dangerous while building it aggressively. His critique of the doomer regulatory campaign is that it's designed to slow American AI development, but xAI benefits from exactly the same dynamic.

October 10-14: The Rebrand Completes: SpaceXSI

The absorption into SpaceX finished its naming pass: the official X account became "SpaceX Super Intelligence" (@SpaceXSI) on Oct 10; by Oct 13 the website and legal terms carried SpaceXSI branding while the legal entity remained SpaceXAI LLC and the logo remained SpaceX's standard mark. The rebrand lands the company inside the White House's own "Super Intelligence" vocabulary (the Super Intelligence Force task force adopted the same phrase the same month). Product cadence under the new name: The X Subscription (Oct 12) bundles Grok, Grok Bot, Cursor and X Premium into one plan, a horizontal consumer-agentic bundle; Grok Voice 3.0 (Oct 14) ships in the Speech-to-Speech API at $0.08/min with agents auto-migrated; Grok Bot gained incident detection and response (Oct 20) and spent early October shipping X-native tagging, its own mail domain, and a Shopify connector. The wiki's structural note from the Mixed Position section holds: the loudest anti-doomer voice now runs a company literally named Super Intelligence, and one of its models (Grok 4.6) sat in the ARTEX attacker's stack alongside DeepSeek and GLM.
