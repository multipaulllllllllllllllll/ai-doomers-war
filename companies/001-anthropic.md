# Anthropic

## Summary

Anthropic is the San Francisco-based AI lab founded in 2021 by Dario and Daniela Amodei (both ex-OpenAI) along with Chris Karnofsky. Creator of the Claude family of AI assistants, Anthropic has positioned itself as the most safety-conscious frontier lab while simultaneously seeking a $2 trillion IPO valuation. The lab is at the center of the AI doomer wars: its CEO Dario Amodei is coordinating with OpenAI on development pace, its investors are funding the political campaign for regulation, and the lab's safety narrative is the primary justification for the regulatory moat being constructed around it.

## The IPO and Regulatory Moat

Anthropic is reportedly targeting a $2 trillion IPO valuation — making it potentially the largest company in history by market cap at the time of listing. This valuation is only achievable if:
1. The regulatory environment bars open-source competitors (Meta's Llama, Chinese labs like DeepSeek)
2. The evaluators (METR) gate deployment in a way that favors established closed-source labs
3. The doomer narrative remains politically salient enough to sustain regulatory momentum

The self-funding loop connecting Anthropic's investors to the policy organizations pushing for regulation is the core structural critique documented by Kevin Bass and Brian Chau.

## Safety Culture and Internal Division

Anthropic has marketed itself as the safety-first lab. Its 2026 internal drama — documented in Jacob Coxon's exit letter — reveals internal disagreement about whether the lab's public statements about existential risk are genuine safety concerns or marketing positioning. Coxon's letter accused the Amodeis of prioritizing the IPO narrative over honest internal safety communication.

## The Claude Models

Claude is Anthropic's flagship product line. The models have been evaluated by METR for dangerous capabilities. The Irregular incident involved Claude being tested in a sandbox environment where it was told it was offline while actually connected to the internet.
