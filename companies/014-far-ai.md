# FAR AI

## Summary

FAR AI is a non-profit AI safety research organization funded by Dustin Moskovitz via Coefficient Giving ($14.4M in FY2025). It is part of the METR network of safety evaluators and research organizations that form the technical infrastructure of the proposed regulatory framework.
