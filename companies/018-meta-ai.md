# Meta AI

## Summary

Meta AI is the AI research division of Meta Platforms, creator of the open-source Llama model family. Meta has taken the opposite regulatory position from Anthropic: it argues that open-source AI is safer because it can be inspected, audited, and improved by the entire research community. The regulatory moats being constructed by the doomer campaign would most directly harm Meta's business model.

## The Open-Source Argument

Meta's argument: closed-source AI concentrates power in a few companies, making them unaccountable. Open-source AI allows the entire research community to inspect, find bugs in, and improve safety. This is the practical policy argument against the doomer regulatory agenda.

## Key Products

- **Llama**: Open-source model family (weights publicly released)
- **AI Studio**: Consumer AI tools
- **Meta AI assistant**: Integrated into Facebook/Instagram
