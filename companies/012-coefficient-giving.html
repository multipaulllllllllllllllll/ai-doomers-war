Coefficient Giving

Summary

Coefficient Giving - formed February 2025 by merging Good Ventures (the Moskovitz/Tuna family foundation) with Open Philanthropy (the grantmaking arm SBF's donations once fed) - is the philanthropic vehicle of Dustin Moskovitz and Cari Tuna. It is the largest private funding stream directed at AI safety organizations in the Effective Altruism ecosystem. Its documented FY2025 grants include $78.8M to Founders Pledge, $14.4M to FAR AI, and $11.5M to Effective Ventures USA - organizations that are part of the METR network ecosystem. Via the National Philanthropic Trust routing, these grants become part of the ~$166M in FY2025 grants to METR's network identified in Kevin Bass's IRS Form 990 audit.

The Funding Network

Coefficient Giving / Good Ventures represents the financial pipeline that connects tech wealth to the AI safety ecosystem. Its grants fund:

  • The organizations that produce safety research (MIRI, Epoch AI)
  • The policy organizations that translate safety research into regulation (ControlAI)
  • The evaluator that gates frontier AI deployments (METR) — via NPT routing

    • The Guardian Story Investigation (Brian Chau, September 15, 2026)

    Brian Chau reported that the same funding network (Moskovitz/Coefficient Giving + Open Society Foundations) sat behind the outlet, journalist, project, and experts in a single Guardian AI safety story. This represents a documented case of a funding network coordinating to function as a media force multiplier - a finding with significant implications for media independence.

    The Self-Amplifying Loop

    Coefficient Giving funding flows into:

  • METR network (via NPT) → METR evaluates Anthropic's models
  • ControlAI → writes regulatory legislation
  • Media amplification → doomer narratives justify METR's role and ControlAI's bills
  • Regulatory moat → Anthropic benefits; IPO valuation increases
  • More philanthropic capital → cycle continues

    • This is the same loop Kevin Bass traced through IRS Form 990 filings - Coefficient Giving is the source of the river, NPT is the tributary system, and METR/ControlAI are the destination organizations.
