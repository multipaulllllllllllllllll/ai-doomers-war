# House joint NVIDIA inquiry

## Involvement

On November 17, chairs [[062-brian-mast|Brian Mast]], [[064-john-moolenaar|John Moolenaar]] and [[078-rick-crawford|Rick Crawford]] jointly announced voluntary transcribed interviews with [[068-jensen-huang|Jensen Huang]] on December 1 and [[071-tim-teter|Tim Teter]] on December 2. The inquiry concerns access to advanced chips and potential national-security risks. This sheet groups the three committees’ shared action; it is not a fourth committee. [House chairs’ joint statement, Nov 17](https://foreignaffairs.house.gov/news/press-releases/jensen-huang-and-nvidia-agree-to-appear-before-congress).
