# U.S. Department of Justice

## Involvement

In their November joint statement, Justice and Commerce traced the handoff to the June 2025 end-use check. Commerce opened the original inquiry; Justice took the criminal lead and said it would continue working with Commerce’s Bureau of Industry and Security. [Commerce and Justice joint statement](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/joint-statement-department-commerce-and-department-justice-megaspeed-investigation).

Singapore’s Home Affairs Ministry separately reported requesting U.S. assistance from Justice and the FBI in obtaining information from a server supplier. It described limited assistance received as of its statement. [Singapore Ministry of Home Affairs](https://www.mha.gov.sg/media-room/newsroom/singapore-investigations-into-alice-huang-huang-le-and-megaspeed-international-pte-ltd/).
