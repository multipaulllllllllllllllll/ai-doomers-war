Super Intelligence Force

White House task force for super intelligence; accord implementation and rogue-incident reporting

Summary

The Super Intelligence Force is the Trump administration's answer to the question its own September accord created: what enforces superintelligence governance when Congress passes nothing. The answer is a task force with the phrase the CEOs signed, chaired by DNI Jay Clayton, run through working groups, and armed with one coercive instrument: the existing enforcement powers of the FTC and DOJ, wielded by a chair (Andrew Ferguson) who sits inside the task force itself.

Structure and Doctrine (October 2026)

  • Chair: DNI Jay Clayton (White House Task Force for Super Intelligence).
  • Working groups (Semafor): accord implementation (Clayton + National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross); incident-reporting protocols and long-term coordination (Defense Undersecretary Emil Michael); law-enforcement and consumer-protection coordination (FTC Chair Andrew Ferguson); critical-infrastructure and workforce partnerships (OPM Director Scott Kupor); a PCAST report commission on AI risk.
  • Members: Vance, Bessent, Hegseth, Kratsios, Klomp, Bisignano, Rudd, Wiles, Walters.
  • Doctrine (The Hill, Oct 20): existing criminal and civil law over new legislation; the FTC's consumer-protection authority "tailor-made," per allies; legal experts doubt it suffices. Prof. Chinmayi Sharma: "People want action now," but no consensus on which laws apply.
  • The reporting mandate: labs told to report rogue behavior immediately and fix it (thenextweb); Axios (Oct 9) ties the mandate to Anthropic's disclosure day.

    • Why the wiki treats this as a structural event

  • It is the enforcement arm of the accord, and the labs' subpoena writer. The Ferguson committee coexists with the FTC's October 15 civil investigative demands to OpenAI and Anthropic, the same agency planning to compel the labs' top executives and METR to testify.
  • It adopts the labs' own vocabulary. "Super Intelligence" was the CEOs' September phrase; the White House made it a proper noun, and xAI rebranded to SpaceXSI under the same banner the same week.
  • It answers the kill-switch contradiction with bureaucracy. The Sept 29 accord (kill switch discussed, no statute) now has a structure whose doctrine is explicitly anti-legislation: existing law, immediate reporting, case-by-case enforcement, in an election month.
