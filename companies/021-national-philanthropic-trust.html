National Philanthropic Trust

Summary

National Philanthropic Trust (NPT) is a donor-advised fund and 501(c)(3) public charity that serves as the routing mechanism for the majority of EA-aligned AI safety philanthropy. Kevin Bass's September 14, 2026 audit used NPT's IRS Form 990 filings - public documents - to reconstruct the complete funding chain from major tech donors through NPT to the METR network of evaluators and researchers. NPT's FY2025 grants (22,258 grants) to METR's ecosystem totaled approximately $166 million.

Why NPT Is Central to the Structure

The significance of NPT is structural: it acts as an intermediary that insulates donor identity and provides tax efficiency while routing large volumes of philanthropic capital to a specific ecosystem of organizations. Donors contribute appreciated assets (stock, equity) to NPT, receive an immediate tax deduction, and NPT then makes grants to the designated recipient organizations over time.

This means:

  • Donors (Coefficient Giving, individual EA philanthropists) contribute to NPT
  • NPT holds and manages the capital, making grants per donor recommendations
  • Grantees (Founders Pledge, RAND Corp, FAR AI, Effective Ventures USA, Epoch AI) receive the grants
  • The grantees form the METR network — the ecosystem that sustains the evaluator and the regulatory advocacy infrastructure

    Public Record

    NPT's IRS Form 990 filings are public record - available via ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer and GuideStar. This is how Bass was able to reconstruct the complete funding map. The 990s disclose total grants by grantee (for grants above the Schedule I threshold), officer compensation, and total assets.
