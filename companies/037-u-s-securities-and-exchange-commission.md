# U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission

## Involvement

On November 20, Reuters cited two people briefed on the matter as saying SEC enforcement staff were examining potential accounting issues at [[019-nvidia|NVIDIA]]. Reuters could not establish when the inquiry began or which accounting issues were involved. This is a reported inquiry, not an SEC finding, charge or restatement. [Reuters, Nov 20](https://www.reuters.com/3fec1b0a85a0/world/us-secs-nvidia-probe-is-examining-potential-accounting-issues-sources-say-2026-11-20/).
