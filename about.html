About This Wiki

What is this?

The chronology begins in June 2026 with Anthropic’s confidential draft filing and Commerce’s model access restrictions. The wiki follows the AI doomer wars that opened in September 2026, when an ex-Anthropic researcher posted a public resignation letter warning that AI could cause human extinction within a decade, and the political, financial, and media ecosystem that mobilized around it. Coverage continues through November 2026: NVIDIA's CEO transition, the Treasury-Commerce fight over H200 licenses to China, Anthropic's Nasdaq listing, and a House staff report on PRC open-weight models in U.S. critical infrastructure.

What's the core thesis?

The structural critique: the same effective altruist donor network that invested in AI labs (Anthropic, OpenAI) also funds the policy organizations (ControlAI, METR, MIRI) and political campaigns pushing for AI regulation. That regulation would create competitive moats for those same labs, raising their valuations and allowing higher IPOs. The doomer narrative, maximum terror about extinction risk, is simultaneously a safety position and a business strategy. Labs fund the fear campaign that makes their own regulatory moat and valuation possible.
