About This Wiki

What is this?

A comprehensive, sourced research wiki covering the AI doomer wars of September 2026 — the crisis that erupted when an ex-Anthropic researcher posted a public resignation letter warning that AI could cause human extinction within a decade, and the political, financial, and media ecosystem that immediately mobilized around it.

What's the core thesis?

The structural critique: the same effective altruist donor network that invested in AI labs (Anthropic, OpenAI) also funds the policy organizations (ControlAI, METR, MIRI) and political campaigns pushing for AI regulation. That regulation would create competitive moats for those same labs, raising their valuations and allowing higher IPOs. The doomer narrative — maximum terror about extinction risk — is simultaneously a safety position and a business strategy. Labs fund the fear campaign that makes their own regulatory moat and valuation possible.
