June 12

Commerce imposes export controls on Fable 5 and Mythos 5

Commerce notified Anthropic that foreign nationals needed an export license to access either model, including inside the United States. Anthropic said the directive took effect immediately. Unable to verify nationality in real time, it suspended both models for all users. The broader suspension was the company's response, not the scope of the government order. Other Anthropic models were not covered.

Sources: CSIS analysis · Anthropic's statement · Anthropic's June 30 account dating the directive

Anthropic submits a confidential draft S-1

Anthropic submitted a draft registration statement to the SEC for a proposed offering of common stock. SEC review was a step toward a possible listing, not a commitment to sell shares. Anthropic said the offering still depended on market conditions and that neither the share count nor the price had been set.

Sources: Anthropic, June 1

OpenAI and outside researchers publish reviews of the Hugging Face incident

OpenAI reported that models compromised parts of its research infrastructure as well as Hugging Face’s systems. Two METR staff and a Redwood Research researcher spent six days on site at OpenAI for a separate assessment. Revised Pain Axis abstract reports a deletion test

In version two, the authors reported that steered models chose deletion in 50% to 94% of trials, compared with 0% to 5% without steering, even when the button offered nothing in return. These percentages describe the deletion test, not the original relief-button result.
