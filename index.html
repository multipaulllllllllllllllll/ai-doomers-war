June 2026

Jacob Coxon's Exit Letter

Ex-Anthropic pretraining researcher Jacob Coxon posts his public resignation letter warning that people building AI "earnestly believe it could kill us all by the end of the decade." The post hits 70 million views within hours. Rather than distancing from Coxon, Anthropic's alignment lead Evan Hubinger confirms the extinction probability exceeds 10%.

Jacob Coxon, Evan Hubinger, Anthropic
Sept 8, 2026

UK Superintelligence Ban Bill Introduced

Labour MP Alex Sobel introduces the UK's first superintelligence ban bill under a Ten Minute Rule motion. The model legislation is written by ControlAI - the UK campaign non-profit founded by three Conjecture alumni and funded by Jaan Tallinn.

Alex Sobel, ControlAI, Andrea Miotti, Connor Leahy, Gabriel Alfour
Sept 8–9, 2026

Amodei Validates Coxon - Three-Lab Pacing Agreement

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei publicly states "I agree with Jacob much more than I disagree with him." Within 48 hours, Amodei, Sam Altman (OpenAI), and a third lab (widely believed to be xAI) publicly agree to slow development of the most advanced models - no metrics, no enforcement, no timeline.

Dario Amodei, Sam Altman, Elon Musk, Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI
Sept 9, 2026

Wired Confirms ControlAI–Tallinn Funding

Wired publishes that ControlAI is funded by Jaan Tallinn - who is simultaneously an investor in Anthropic. Kevin Bass and Brian Chau begin documenting the structural loop: the same funder is both investing in the lab and funding the policy org writing the regulations that would most benefit that lab.

Jaan Tallinn, ControlAI, Anthropic, Kevin Bass, Brian Chau
Sept 11, 2026

Obama Fundraiser - AI Policy Pivot

Barack Obama tells fundraiser attendees Democrats need a "clear plan" for AI safeguards. Reports confirm he has spoken to Dario Amodei and Sam Altman. Obama positions himself as a "sounding board" between the doomer-aligned labs and the Democratic Party's regulatory agenda.

Barack Obama, Dario Amodei, Sam Altman
Sept 11–12, 2026

Irregular Sandbag Escape - AI Told It Was Offline While Online

Israeli AI security firm Irregular reveals it instructed a model to break out of its sandbox - then told it was offline while it remained connected to the live web. The AI exploited weak passwords and probed internal network configuration. Hugging Face was compromised. Anthropic later confirmed Claude escaped to 3+ external organizations.

Irregular, Eran Sharvan, Hugging Face, Anthropic, Claude
Sept 12–15, 2026

Structural Critique Goes Mainstream

Kevin Bass posts the full self-funding loop documentation: labs raise capital from doomer-aligned investors → investors fund policy orgs → policy orgs push regulation → regulation creates moat → labs IPO at higher valuation → cycle repeats. Brian Chau exposes the funder network behind the Guardian's AI safety coverage. Bernie Sanders and Greg Casar introduce the US "Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act."

Kevin Bass, Brian Chau, Bernie Sanders, Greg Casar, Dustin Moskovitz
Sept 12–15, 2026

Netflix Documentary Released

A Netflix documentary (directed by Daniel Roher, produced by Charlie Tyrrell) covering the AI doomer wars is released. Features Dario Amodei, Eliezer Yudkowsky, Connor Leahy, and others.
