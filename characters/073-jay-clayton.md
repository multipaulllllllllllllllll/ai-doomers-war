# Jay Clayton

## Involvement

The Senate confirmed Clayton as director of national intelligence on July 28, according to Reuters. [Reuters, Jul 28](https://www.reuters.com/world/us-senate-confirms-trump-nominee-clayton-nations-top-spy-2026-07-28/).

Asked on ABC about [[069-alice-huang|Alice Huang]] and [[033-megaspeed-international|Megaspeed]] on November 22, Clayton said the investigation into her and her firm was ongoing, CNBC reported. He did not announce a verdict or charges. [CNBC, Nov 23](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/nvidia-megaspeed-chipmaker-us.html).
