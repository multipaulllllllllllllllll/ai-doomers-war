# Tim Scott

## Involvement

The Hill reported that Scott set a December 3 hearing for [[068-jensen-huang|Jensen Huang]] after his return as [[019-nvidia|NVIDIA]] CEO. [The Hill, Nov 23](https://thehill.com/homenews/senate/6238064-nvidia-ceo-ai-hearing-december/).

The committee notice lists Huang as the witness and specifies an open session on U.S. export controls. This was a scheduled appearance as of November 23. [Senate Banking hearing notice](https://www.banking.senate.gov/hearings/protecting-americas-ai-advantage-oversight-of-us-export-controls).
