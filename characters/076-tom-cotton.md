# Tom Cotton

## Involvement

On October 13, 2025, Cotton wrote to Commerce Secretary [[060-howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]] asking the department to investigate [[033-megaspeed-international|Megaspeed]]’s ownership, affiliations and ties to U.S. chip suppliers. His letter cited press reports; it records a request, not a determination that diversion occurred. [Cotton letter, Oct 13, 2025](https://www.cotton.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/media/doc/101325lutnickletter.pdf).

The departments’ later chronology dates the start of Commerce’s investigation to 2024, before Cotton’s letter. [Commerce and Justice joint statement](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/joint-statement-department-commerce-and-department-justice-megaspeed-investigation).
