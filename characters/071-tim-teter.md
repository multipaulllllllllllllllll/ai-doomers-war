# Tim Teter

## Involvement

On November 17, the chairs of three House committees announced that Teter had voluntarily agreed to a transcribed interview about [[019-nvidia|NVIDIA]] and access to advanced chips. The committees scheduled Teter for December 2, one day after [[068-jensen-huang|Jensen Huang]]. Their announcement records an agreement to appear, not testimony or a finding against Teter. [House chairs’ joint statement, Nov 17](https://foreignaffairs.house.gov/news/press-releases/jensen-huang-and-nvidia-agree-to-appear-before-congress).
