# Jim Himes

## Involvement

In a November 20 letter to [[elon-musk|Elon Musk]], Himes requested a briefing on October disruptions to the Starshield service in Ukraine. He asked which areas had service restored and whether [[039-spacex|SpaceX]] or a government official had directed the changes. The letter asks who made the decision; it does not establish the answer. [Himes letter, Nov 20](https://democrats-intelligence.house.gov/uploadedfiles/11.20.12_letter_from_hpsci_ranking_member_jim_himes_to_musk.pdf).
