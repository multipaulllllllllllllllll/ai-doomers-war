# Jacob Coxon

## Summary

Jacob Coxon was a pretraining researcher at Anthropic who resigned in September 2026 and posted a public exit letter on X that became the spark for the entire AI doomer wars episode. His letter — claiming that AI builders "earnestly believe" extinction within the decade — hit approximately 70 million views within days and triggered a cascade of media coverage, public statements from Anthropic leadership, and a three-lab CEO agreement on development pacing. Coxon's letter provided the justification for a series of coordinated responses from Anthropic, its competitors, and the political apparatus aligned with AI safety regulation.

## The Exit Letter

Coxon's letter, posted September 8, 2026, made the claim that researchers inside frontier AI labs privately hold beliefs about catastrophic and extinction-level risk from AI that they do not state publicly. The letter did not reveal proprietary information but rather described what he characterized as common private beliefs among AI builders. He announced his resignation and went public with the warning rather than staying inside to advocate.

## The Cascade

The letter's impact was immediate:

- **Evan Hubinger** (Anthropic alignment lead) publicly confirmed the >10% extinction probability within 24 hours
- **Dario Amodei** publicly stated agreement with Coxon's framing
- Within 48 hours, three rival AI lab CEOs publicly agreed to a development "pause" framework
- The letter provided the political occasion for an existing coordination plan to be announced publicly

The speed of the response from multiple labs — coordinated within approximately 48 hours across competitors who had previously shown no public alignment — is a key element of the structural critique documented in this wiki. The Coxon letter provided the occasion; the coordination was pre-arranged.

## Key Quote

> "The people building AI earnestly believe that it could kill us all by the end of the decade."
