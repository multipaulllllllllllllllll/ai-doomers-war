Andrew Garbarino

Chairman, House Homeland Security Committee

Summary

Andrew Garbarino chairs the House Homeland Security Committee. With john-moolenaar he is the named co-releaser of the November 20, 2026 joint investigative report on PRC open-weight models. The wiki's earlier GLM-5.3 / DeepSeek distillation track now has a House homeland product attached to it: get the models off federal systems and off high-risk infrastructure.

November 20, 2026: Joint investigative report

The Homeland Security press release (Chairmen Garbarino, Moolenaar release joint investigative report urging removal of PRC open-weight models from federal systems, high-risk critical infrastructure) is the primary. Four findings are on the page:

  1. PRC open-weight models are in U.S. government systems
  2. They are in high-risk critical infrastructure
  3. They have demonstrated advanced capabilities with minimal safeguards
  4. They collect data that can be accessed by the PRC

Fourteen recommendations follow. Their titles were not copied into the research log and are not invented here. Readers should open the House page rather than treat this sheet as a substitute for the PDF.

The report is a removal demand, not an export-control memo. It sits next to Mast's NVIDIA H200/AMD MI325X license disclosure as the model-weights half of the House China-policy week. The separate Megaspeed dispute should not be reduced to a Lutnick hawk position: Axios describes a Rubio-drafted memo handed to Lutnick and sources alleging Lutnick resisted controls; Commerce denied the reported Bessent confrontation. Axios, November 11.
