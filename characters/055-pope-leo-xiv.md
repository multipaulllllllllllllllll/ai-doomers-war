# Pope Leo XIV

## Summary

Pope Leo XIV (Robert Prevost, OSA, elected May 2025, the first American pope) has turned the Catholic Church into the **single largest moral actor in the AI doomer war**, and the first institution with standing on both sides of the "hoax" divide: 1.4 billion faithful, a scholarly encyclical tradition, and zero commercial incentives that the labs can claim to dismiss.

His move-set this wiki documents: (1) **magnifica humanitas**, the first encyclical ever devoted to a technology question as its core, signed on the 135th anniversary of *Rerum Novarum*'s answer to the Industrial Revolution; (2) **the Olah co-launch**, presenting it personally with Anthropic's interpretability chief, an "unusual" pairing a Vatican official said signaled seriousness; (3) **the plane presser**, the first time a pope has directly, by name, rebutted a sitting U.S. president on AI, days before midterms.

## The Papal Plane vs. the UN Podium (September 28, 2026)

Aboard the flight home from a four-day France trip, freshly reading that afternoon's **NVIDIA Open Agent Safety Platform** news, Leo was asked about U.S.–China responsibility for AI catastrophic risk:

> "This is a problem that, I think, we need to sit down and talk about. **We can't just sit back and pretend nothing is going to happen.**"
>
> "If someone were to ask me 'Am I in panic mode?' No, I'm not. **I sleep at night.** I do think that the concerns raised by many of the experts, specialists in AI **should be taken seriously.**"
>
> "I don't think that that is **'fake news' as some have said** to try and cause whether financial or other some other kind of benefit."

On Jensen Huang, read the guardrails announcement, then the rebuttal: **"He's the same one, however, that says there should be no limits placed and no government regulation."**

Targeting Trump's UN General Assembly "hoax" line (which a White House official told Axios was aimed at Democrats "scoring points before the midterms"), Leo reframed the entire epistemic fight: the question isn't whether doomers are a lobbying cover, it's whether experts are credible, and the pope says they are. This is the doomer movement's strongest **legitimacy recruit** yet: a voice whose motive can't be dismissed as regulatory-moat-seeking, equity positions, or midterm tactics.

## The Encyclical: *Magnifica Humanitas* (May 2026)

"On safeguarding the human person in the time of artificial intelligence." Full text at vatican.va. Key positions carried into the war's policy debates:

- AI must **serve humanity, not concentrate power**, denounces the "concentration of power in the digital world" (a direct hit on the [[Nvidia]]-[[Anthropic]]-[[OpenAI]] capex triangle and the $2T prospectus story)
- **Government regulation** of AI companies explicitly advocated, the church not asking for caution but for authority
- **Autonomous weapons**: "increasingly autonomous weapons systems practically beyond any human reach to govern them effectively"
- **Algorithmic redlining**: algorithms that "block access to healthcare, employment and security on the basis of data tainted by prejudice and injustice"
- **Data colonialism**: "new forms of slavery and colonialism" turning personal life into exploitable information, the digital environment as a "space of exploitation" (§178-179)
- **Nuclear framing**: AI compared to nuclear energy, "now demands to be **disarmed**" (Vatican News reflection)
- Neither "a force antagonistic to humanity" nor "inherently evil" (but **"never neutral"** (Brookings analysis panel))
- "Humanity, in all its grandeur and woundedness, must never be replaced or surpassed."

## The Olah Question (the strange bridge)

The pope chose **Christopher Olah**, Anthropic co-founder, head of interpretability, 33, self-described atheist, as his lay co-presenter. In that speech Olah told the Synod Hall that his team "keeps finding things that are mysterious, even unsettling... evidence of introspection... internal states that functionally mirror joy, satisfaction, fear, grief, and unease. I don't know what that means." The Vatican thereby gave the world's most prominent **inside-lab model-welfare signal** a papal stage four months before [[Reciprocal Research]]'s Pain Axis paper made the same claim empirically, and the same pope who platformed that uncertainty now demands the opposite of the labs' posture: **regulation, not acceleration**. The doomer war's three fronts (liability/Treasury, engineering/NVIDIA, moral/Vatican) now meet at the welfare question the papal-Olah pairing planted.

## Key Facts
- **Born:** Robert Francis Prevost, 1955, Chicago; Augustinian order; served in Peru; headed the Dicastery for Bishops before election
- **Elected:** May 2025 (first American pope; chose the name of Leo XIII, social-encyclical pope)
- **Encyclical:** *Magnifica Humanitas*, signed May 15, 2026; released/presented May 25, 2026 (first pope to personally present an encyclical)
- **AI Commission:** Vatican body promoting dialogue/study "to find ways to make sure that AI does not get to a point of destroying humanity or removing humanity from the conversation"
- **Stance on doom:** "relatively optimistic" solutions findable "outside the questions... of power, who gets there first"
- **Prior Trump conflicts:** U.S.-Israeli war in Iran (April back-and-forth); migration crackdown; AI is the third front
