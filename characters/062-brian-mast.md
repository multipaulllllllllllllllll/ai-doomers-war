# Brian Mast

## Summary

Brian Mast is chairman of the House Foreign Affairs Committee. The tenth sweep records him as the member who put BIS H200 license numbers on the public record: how many chips were requested, how many were approved, and which Chinese names sat on the destination list. That release is a primary document, not a newspaper paraphrase.

## The H200 license release

The House Foreign Affairs press release (Chairman Brian Mast exercises legislative authority to publicly release AI chip license information for China) published figures from a BIS May 11 summary:

- About 3.44 million H200 chips requested
- 879,868 approved
- 75,000 for Alibaba
- 150,000 for Tencent
- Maginfra and Beijing New Matrix named (possible installers / suspected smuggling fronts in the committee's framing)

NVIDIA said it followed then-current rules and stopped H200 shipments to China. This sheet does not add remaining Maginfra unit counts that were not locked against the House page in the research log.

The release lands in the same November window as Lutnick's Megaspeed memo, Trump's "probably" overrule, and the November 19 BIS remote-access rule. Mast is the Hill node; [[howard-lutnick]] is the Cabinet node; both sit on [[china-hawks]].

## What to Watch

- Whether the 879,868 approved figure becomes the denominator in a restoration fight if Trump puts H200s back on the table with Xi
- Whether Maginfra and Beijing New Matrix get Entity List follow-up from [[us-commerce]] or stay as named rows in a committee PDF
