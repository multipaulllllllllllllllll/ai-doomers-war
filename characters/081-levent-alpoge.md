# Levent Alpöge

## Involvement

The Lean FRO and Mathlib community reported that Alpöge released a Lean library on November 20. They distinguished compiling the code from verifying what it proved: two tools compared the theorem in the library with Mathlib’s independent statement of the Riemann Hypothesis. Their statement supports the proof attribution, not a laboratory affiliation. [Lean FRO and Mathlib community](https://leanprover-community.github.io/blog/posts/riemann-hypothesis/).
