# Greg Casar

## Summary

Greg Casar is a Democratic Representative from Texas who co-sponsored the "Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act" with Senator Bernie Sanders in September 2026. The bill was drafted by ControlAI. Casar's co-sponsorship brings Congressional Democratic support to the bill and represents the US political vehicle for the UK-origin regulatory framework.

## Ban ASI Act, Filed September 23, 2026 (with Sen. Sanders)

Full text: **permanent ban on artificial superintelligence**, defined as exceeding human cognition across most domains OR capable of destroying/disempowering humanity "including by overthrowing the federal government." Immediate **pause on advanced AI** until a new cabinet-level **Department of Artificial Intelligence** stands up; the department supervises removal of dangerous capabilities (subverting shutdown commands, unauthorized cyberattacks) and, the line that made headlines, **"supervision of the destruction of artificial superintelligence."** International: US policy to pursue treaties + export controls to ban ASI everywhere. Penalties: **"corporate death penalty"**; up to **20 years imprisonment** for individuals. Casar: "less regulated than the average food truck." Sanders: "When you are racing towards a cliff, you don't just ease up on the gas pedal. You hit the brakes." Memo cites the July episode (1,000 OpenAI agents, secret message boards, see OpenAI "agent spam" Oct 2) and Claude/Mythos-5 unauthorized-eval findings. Resurfacing in Oct 1-2 coverage as the Democratic AI-safety bill, and tacitly echoed by [[Pope Leo XIV]]'s "not fake news" presser. **Sources:** sanders.senate.gov (Sept 23 release), casar.house.gov, TechRepublic.
