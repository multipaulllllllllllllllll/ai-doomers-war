John Moolenaar

Chairman, House Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party

Summary

John Moolenaar chairs the House Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party. He is the China-committee co-chair of the November 20, 2026 open-weight report. andrew-garbarino is the homeland co-chair. The Select Committee is the long-running CCP vehicle; this sheet records the November 20 product, not a full committee history.

November 20, 2026: Joint investigative report

Same four findings as the Garbarino page, taken from the Homeland Security release:

  1. PRC open-weight models are in U.S. government systems
  2. They are in high-risk critical infrastructure
  3. They have demonstrated advanced capabilities with minimal safeguards
  4. They collect data that can be accessed by the PRC

The report asks for removal from federal systems and high-risk critical infrastructure. It is the model-weights companion to Mast's NVIDIA H200/AMD MI325X license disclosure and the reported Rubio-drafted Megaspeed memo handed to Lutnick. Axios describes Lutnick resisting controls, not authoring the memo; Commerce denied the reported Bessent confrontation. Axios, November 11. Anthropic's September 30 GLM-5.3 report (Zhipu open model as a documented cyber capability) is the labs' earlier version of the same claim. This House product does not cite that lab report in the recovered press text, and this sheet does not pretend it does.

What to Watch

  • Whether the Select Committee follows with entity-list or procurement language, or stays at "urge removal"
  • Whether DeepSeek, GLM, or other named weights appear in a later table
