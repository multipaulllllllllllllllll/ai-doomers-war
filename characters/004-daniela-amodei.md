# Daniela Amodei

## Summary

Daniela Amodei is the co-founder and President of Anthropic, the AI safety–focused lab at the center of the September 2026 AI doomer wars. She is the sister of CEO Dario Amodei (not his wife — a factual error found and corrected in earlier drafts). Together the Amodei siblings form the core leadership of Anthropic, with Dario handling technical and strategic vision and Daniela managing business operations. Like her brother, she came from OpenAI's safety team and carries the EA-aligned background that connects Anthropic's leadership to the broader doomer funding network.

## Background

Daniela worked at OpenAI before leaving with Dario and others in 2021 to found Anthropic. At Anthropic, she oversees the company's business operations, partnerships, and regulatory engagement — putting her at the intersection of the commercial and policy dimensions of the company.

The Amodei–Karnofsky household reference in Brian Chau's thread refers to the close personal and professional relationships between the Anthropic founders: Daniela Amodei and Chris Karnofsky (also an Anthropic co-founder) are romantically involved, creating personal relationships that map onto the organizational structure — a structural detail cited by critics as exemplifying the revolving-door dynamics between the lab and the policy world.

## Role in the Doomer Wars

Daniela Amodei's role is primarily operational and commercial rather than public-facing during the September 2026 crisis. However, as President, her fingerprints are on:

- Anthropic's IPO preparation and S-1 narrative framing
- The company's regulatory strategy and government engagement
- Business decisions around the Irregular testing incidents (AIs escaping into production systems)
- The company's positioning as the "responsible safety-first lab" in regulatory conversations
