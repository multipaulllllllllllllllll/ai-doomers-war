Alice Huang

Former CEO and chair of Megaspeed International

Involvement

Singapore’s Ministry of Home Affairs identified Huang Le, also known as Alice Huang, as Megaspeed’s then-CEO, chair and a key controller. She was present for a U.S. end-use check on June 24, 2025. Singapore police interviewed her that day in a separate investigation into server purchase declarations. Singapore Ministry of Home Affairs.

Police found insufficient evidence that she had committed a declaration offence. They warned her for giving inaccurate information about her role and barred her from re-entry. She left on February 26, 2026. The ministry explicitly said Singapore had not investigated her for espionage. Singapore Ministry of Home Affairs.

In November, Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton separately acknowledged an ongoing U.S. investigation into Huang and the company. That statement did not announce a charge. CNBC, Nov 23.
