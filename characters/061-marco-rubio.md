# Marco Rubio

## Summary

Marco Rubio is Secretary of State and National Security Adviser in Axios's November reporting. He is the reported drafter of a memo outlining Megaspeed concerns, handed to [[Howard Lutnick]], not merely a supporter of a Commerce-authored memo. Sources describe Rubio and [[Scott Bessent]] supporting controls that Lutnick resisted. Their alignment should not be reversed.

**Sources:** [Axios, November 10](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/10/lutnick-nvidia-jensen-huang-china-memo); [Axios, Situation Room, November 11](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/11/bessent-lutnick-megaspeed-nvidia-china-chips); [Axios, Oval Office, November 11](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/11/trump-lutnick-nvidia-china-chip-export-controls).

## May-November 2026: Intelligence warning and Megaspeed memo

Axios reported on November 10 that Lutnick received a classified warning in May about Jensen Huang's relationship with Alice Huang, whom U.S. intelligence suspected of being a Chinese agent. Rubio oversaw the drafting, according to the account relayed by Axios from the New York Times. Separately, Axios's November 11 account describes a Rubio-drafted memo outlining concerns about Megaspeed, handed to Lutnick. The retrieved reporting does not establish that these were identical documents.

According to Axios's anonymous-source reporting, at the November 5 Situation Room meeting [[Scott Bessent]] pressed Lutnick about Megaspeed's absence from the Entity List, and Lutnick said he would look into it. An unnamed source disparaged Lutnick's response; that was not Bessent's characterization of a memo. Commerce denied that the confrontation occurred.

Axios separately reported that Rubio, Bessent, Greer and Hegseth supported controls; Trump consulted [[Elon Musk]], who supported their case. Lutnick reportedly warned that action would start a trade war with China, and proposed further options after Trump approved action. This is attributed reporting about resistance to controls, not evidence that Lutnick authored a hawkish memo. The reports do not substantiate a Bessent liar quotation or a presidential probable-override quotation.

**Sources:** [Axios, November 10](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/10/lutnick-nvidia-jensen-huang-china-memo); [Axios, Situation Room, November 11](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/11/bessent-lutnick-megaspeed-nvidia-china-chips); [Axios, Oval Office, November 11](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/11/trump-lutnick-nvidia-china-chip-export-controls).

The New York Times English URL for its Rubio-warning story is dated November 8, not November 12. The full body was not retrieved for this implementation and is not quoted here. The May warning and the Megaspeed memo remain separately described reports, not presumed identical texts.

## What to Watch

The reported presidential approvals and interagency support do not establish an implemented Entity List action. Follow-up requires a published agency decision and the outcome of ongoing investigations, not an inferred presidential override.
