Jakub Pachocki

OpenAI chief scientist and author of a proposed model-progress measure

Involvement

In a November 22 OpenAI essay, Pachocki published internal pretraining measurements and proposed comparing developers’ progress using perplexity on held-out data. Lower perplexity means a model predicts the next token better on that data. He argued the measure could inform shared safety standards; the essay was a proposal, not an agreement among labs. OpenAI, Nov 22.
