# Jensen Huang

## Involvement

On November 12, [[019-nvidia|NVIDIA]] announced Huang’s resignation and named CFO [[059-colette-kress|Colette Kress]] interim CEO. [[070-harvey-c-jones|Harvey C. Jones]] became the board’s first chair. [NVIDIA transition, Nov 12](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition).

On November 23, the board voted unanimously to restore Huang as president, CEO and director. It said it had spoken with Huang and received input from senior U.S. officials. Its assessment that his return posed no national security risk was the board’s conclusion, not a finding by investigators. [NVIDIA reinstatement, Nov 23](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director).

Huang agreed to a December 1 transcribed interview before three House committees. [House chairs’ joint statement, Nov 17](https://foreignaffairs.house.gov/news/press-releases/jensen-huang-and-nvidia-agree-to-appear-before-congress).

Senate Banking separately scheduled him as the witness at its December 3 public hearing on export controls. [Senate Banking hearing notice](https://www.banking.senate.gov/hearings/protecting-americas-ai-advantage-oversight-of-us-export-controls).
