# Brian Chau

## Summary

Brian Chau is a journalist and researcher behind Effort News who produced the most detailed structural mapping of the EA/doomer funding network in September 2026. His thread (September 14, 2026, 125K impressions, 291+ retweets) mapped the "way too coincidental" convergence of the same small network of EA-aligned investors and operatives across AI labs raising alarms, the organizations calling for regulation, the proposed regulatory structures, and the political lobby backing them. He followed up (September 15) with reporting that the same funding network (Moskovitz/Coefficient Giving + Open Society Foundations) sat behind the outlet, journalist, project, and experts in a single Guardian AI safety story — a documented case of funding network coordination functioning as a media force multiplier.
