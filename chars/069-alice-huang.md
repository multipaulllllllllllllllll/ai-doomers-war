# Alice Huang

## Involvement

Singapore’s Ministry of Home Affairs identified Huang Le, also known as Alice Huang, as [[033-megaspeed-international|Megaspeed]]’s then-CEO, chair and a key controller. She was present for a U.S. end-use check on June 24, 2025. Singapore police interviewed her that day in a separate investigation into server purchase declarations. [Singapore Ministry of Home Affairs](https://www.mha.gov.sg/media-room/newsroom/singapore-investigations-into-alice-huang-huang-le-and-megaspeed-international-pte-ltd/).

Police found insufficient evidence that she had committed a declaration offence. They warned her for giving inaccurate information about her role and barred her from re-entry. She left on February 26, 2026. The ministry explicitly said Singapore had not investigated her for espionage. [Singapore Ministry of Home Affairs](https://www.mha.gov.sg/media-room/newsroom/singapore-investigations-into-alice-huang-huang-le-and-megaspeed-international-pte-ltd/).

In November, Director of National Intelligence [[073-jay-clayton|Jay Clayton]] separately acknowledged an ongoing U.S. investigation into Huang and the company. That statement did not announce a charge. [CNBC, Nov 23](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/nvidia-megaspeed-chipmaker-us.html).
