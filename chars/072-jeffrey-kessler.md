# Jeffrey Kessler

## Involvement

Axios reported that officials repeatedly warned Kessler about [[033-megaspeed-international|Megaspeed]] in meetings and that he deferred a decision to Secretary [[060-howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]]. This is Axios’s account of internal deliberations. [Axios, Nov 11](https://www.axios.com/2026/11/11/bessent-lutnick-megaspeed-nvidia-china-chips).

Commerce and Justice confirmed the export-control investigation and the transfer of the criminal lead to Justice. Their statement does not attribute the transfer to Kessler. [Commerce and Justice joint statement](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/joint-statement-department-commerce-and-department-justice-megaspeed-investigation).
