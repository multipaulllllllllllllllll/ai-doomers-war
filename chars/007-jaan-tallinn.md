# Jaan Tallinn

## Summary

Jaan Tallinn is the Skype co-founder who has become the most structurally significant figure in the AI doomer network — simultaneously an investor in the lab being regulated (Anthropic), a funder of the political campaign pushing for that regulation (ControlAI), and a donor to organizations connected to the evaluator being positioned as the quasi-independent referee (CSER/METR). Kevin Bass's September 14, 2026 financial audit — tracing dollars through National Philanthropic Trust and IRS Form 990 filings — elevated the critique from narrative to quantified financial map. His position is the structural core of the critique: the same person benefits from regulatory moats around Anthropic and funds the organizations advocating for those moats.

## Investment in Anthropic

Tallinn led Anthropic's Series A in 2021. His investment in Anthropic means he directly benefits from any regulatory moat that makes it harder for competitors to enter the frontier AI market. Anthropic's Series H (May 28, 2026) added Jane Street and D.E. Shaw Ventures as investors. This is not a conflict of interest in the simple sense — it is a structural feature of the entire network map.

## Funding ControlAI

Per TIME's investigation (September 8, 2026) and confirmed by Wired (September 9), Tallinn is a supporter of ControlAI — the campaign non-profit that wrote both the UK and US superintelligence ban bills. ControlAI was founded by Conjecture alumni including Andrea Miotti and Connor Leahy. Tallinn's funding of ControlAI means he is funding the political campaign that would create the regulatory structure most beneficial to his Anthropic investment.

## The NPT Connection (Kevin Bass Audit)

Kevin Bass's September 14, 2026 audit traced the funding chain through National Philanthropic Trust (NPT). NPT's FY2025 grants (July 2024–June 2025) to METR's network totaled approximately $166M:

This is the same philanthropic infrastructure — rooted in the EA network where Tallinn is a prominent figure — that sustains the organizations advocating for AI regulation.

## MIRI and the Research Pipeline

Tallinn is a longtime donor to the Machine Intelligence Research Institute (MIRI), the AI safety research organization that has trained many researchers who later cycled through frontier labs and METR. MIRI represents the pipeline: donors like Tallinn fund safety research → researchers train at MIRI → researchers move to labs and METR → labs advocate for regulation → regulation benefits labs → donors' equity increases.

## Centre for the Study of Existential Risk (CSER)

Tallinn is a principal at CSER, Cambridge University. CSER is one of the foundational EA x-risk organizations and has connections to METR through shared funding and personnel networks.
