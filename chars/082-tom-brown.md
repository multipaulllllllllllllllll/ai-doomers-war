# Tom Brown

## Involvement

Reuters reported that Brown represented [[001-anthropic|Anthropic]] at a U.S.-hosted G20 technology event in August and appeared at a government AI tool launch in September. On November 20 Reuters identified him, citing a person familiar with the plan, as a prospective Trump Accounts donor. Scott Bessent had described an unnamed founder; neither a completed gift nor a specific amount follows from that account. [Reuters, Nov 20](https://www.reuters.com/5f374b32b07b/world/us/anthropic-founder-make-large-gift-trump-accounts-bessent-says-2026-11-20/).
