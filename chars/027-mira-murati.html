Summary

Mira Murati was the CTO of OpenAI who resigned in September 2025 after reportedly pushing internally for a 6-month pause on AI development. Her resignation was framed as a safety-aligned departure - a senior technical leader breaking with leadership over disagreement about development pace.

Significance

Murati's departure represented the internal safety division at OpenAI - the possibility that even at the most senior technical levels, there were disagreements about whether the current development pace was responsible. Her reported push for a 6-month pause is consistent with the doomer position.
