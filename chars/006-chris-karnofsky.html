Chris Karnofsky

Summary

Chris Karnofsky is a co-founder of Anthropic, part of the small group that left OpenAI in 2021 with Dario and Daniela Amodei. He is romantically involved with Daniela Amodei, creating the "Amodei–Karnofsky household" reference in the Brian Chau thread. This personal relationship - mapping onto Anthropic's leadership structure - is cited by critics as exemplifying the revolving-door dynamics between Anthropic's personnel and the policy organizations that advocate for its preferred regulation.

Role in the Anthropic EA Network

Karnofsky's significance in the network map is primarily structural: his relationship to Daniela places him at the intersection of Anthropic's leadership and the EA network that connects Anthropic to the policy world. The "Amodei–Karnofsky household" phrase captures the dual personal/professional network that characterizes the Israeli EA organizing network and its connections to the San Francisco–based Anthropic leadership.
