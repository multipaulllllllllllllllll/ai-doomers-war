Billy Perrigo

Summary

Billy Perrigo is a staff writer at TIME who produced the September 8, 2026 investigation "The Growing Push to Ban Superintelligent AI" - the primary source establishing that ControlAI (founded by Conjecture alumni) had written both the UK and US superintelligence ban bills. His reporting confirmed the structural relationship between the doomer policy organizations and the political campaigns.

Key Reporting

The TIME investigation established:

  • ControlAI wrote both the UK bill (introduced by Alex Sobel) and the US bill (sponsored by Bernie Sanders)
  • ControlAI is supported by Jaan Tallinn (Skpye co-founder, Anthropic investor)
  • ControlAI was founded by Andrea Miotti, Connor Leahy, and Gabriel Alfour — all Conjecture alumni
  • The bills seek a global treaty banning superintelligent AI development

    • Significance

    Perrigo's reporting provided the factual foundation for the regulatory capture critique - establishing that the legislative language was not coming from Congressional staff or independent policy analysis but from a single organization with financial ties to the lab that would most benefit from regulation.
