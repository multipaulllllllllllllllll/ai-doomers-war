Summary

Brian Chau is a journalist and researcher behind Effort News who produced the most detailed structural mapping of the EA/doomer funding network in September 2026. His thread (Sept 14, 2026, 125K impressions, 291+ RTs) mapped the "way too coincidental" convergence of the same small network of EA-aligned investors and operatives across AI labs raising alarms, the organizations calling for regulation, the proposed regulatory structures, and the political lobby backing them.

Key Findings

The EA Network Map (Sept 14 thread)

Chau's thread identified the following nodes in the EA network:

  • Moskovitz/Tuna philanthropy (Open Phil / Coefficient Giving)
  • The Amodei–Karnofsky household
  • The Nevo brothers (Israeli EA network)
  • Israeli EA organizers who built both the AI safety orgs and the regulatory campaign organizations

    • Guardian Story Investigation (Sept 15)

    Chau's most significant finding: the same funding network (Moskovitz/Coefficient Giving + Open Society Foundations) sat behind the outlet, journalist, project, and experts in a single Guardian AI safety story. This represents a documented case of funding network coordination functioning as a media force multiplier - a finding with significant implications for media independence.

    Key Quote

    "I reported last week on these firms that are all paid by one of two foundations: Dustin Moskovitz's Open Philanthropy [now Coefficient Giving] and the Open Society Foundations."

    Significance

    Chau's work represents the investigative journalism thread of the doomer wars - using publicly available information about funding and organizational connections to document the structural relationships underlying the regulatory campaign. His findings provide the evidentiary basis for the regulatory capture critique.
