# Brian Mast

## Summary

Brian Mast chairs the House Foreign Affairs Committee. The committee's November 19, 2026 disclosure published a BIS May 11 summary and underlying license table covering NVIDIA H200 and AMD MI325X chips. Its requested and approved counts must not be described as NVIDIA-only totals or actual deliveries.

**Sources:** [HFAC summary, November 19](https://democrats-foreignaffairs.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/11.19.26-ECRA-Information-Disclosure-HFAC-Summary.pdf); [BIS table as of May 11](https://democrats-foreignaffairs.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/11.19.26-ECRA-Information-Disclosure-of-BIS-License-Decisions.pdf).

## November 19, 2026: H200 and MI325X disclosure

The House Foreign Affairs Committee's November 19 disclosure of a BIS May 11 summary covers NVIDIA H200 and AMD MI325X chips together:

- 3,444,160 covered chips requested; 879,868 approved across 21 applications.
- Requests for Alibaba and subsidiaries totaled 948,950 covered chips; the approved Alibaba figure was 75,000. The summary reports 150,000 approved for Tencent and subsidiaries across the covered products, not all NVIDIA.
- Separate applications not fully reflected in the May summary include NVIDIA's December 19, 2025 request for 445,000 H200s for Tencent. A request is not an approval.
- All but two NVIDIA applications list Maginfra, Beijing New Matrix, ZTE Kangxun and Lenovo or subsidiaries as entities that may install systems in data centers. These are potential installers, not necessarily buyers. Some named entities appear as ultimate consignees in AMD applications.
- Every NVIDIA and AMD application stated that the proposed transfer posed a low diversion risk. That is the applications' characterization, not an independent risk finding.

The underlying table marks NVIDIA's March 16 applications for Tesla (Shanghai), 4,000 chips; BYD, 4,000; and Great Wall Motor, 4,800, as "Approved, not shipped." That status is as of the May 11 table, not proof of subsequent deliveries. CBS separately reported Kress's November 17 statement that NVIDIA had stopped shipping licensed Hopper data-center products to China; it does not convert every approval into a shipment.

**Sources:** [HFAC summary, November 19](https://democrats-foreignaffairs.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/11.19.26-ECRA-Information-Disclosure-HFAC-Summary.pdf); [BIS table as of May 11](https://democrats-foreignaffairs.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/11.19.26-ECRA-Information-Disclosure-of-BIS-License-Decisions.pdf); [CBS, earnings call](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/nvidia-stops-shipping-hopper-chips-china/).

The Washington Post reported almost 580,000 NVIDIA chips approved, distinct from the combined 879,868 NVIDIA/AMD total. **Source:** [Washington Post, November 20](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/11/20/officials-approved-sale-580000-nvidia-chips-chinese-companies/).

## What to Watch

Subsequent license decisions, shipments and any Entity List action require separate documentation. The 879,868 approved total cannot be used as a denominator for NVIDIA shipments. The reported Rubio memo and the disputed Bessent-Lutnick exchange are separate from this documentary license record.
