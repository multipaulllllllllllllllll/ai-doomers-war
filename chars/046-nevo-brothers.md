# The Nevo Brothers

## Summary

The Nevo Brothers are Israeli brothers who serve as key organizers in the Effective Altruism network, connecting the Israeli AI safety and EA organizing scene to the broader doomer funding ecosystem that includes Anthropic leadership and the Moskovitz/Tuna philanthropy network. They were identified in Brian Chau's September 14 EA network mapping thread.

## Significance

The Israeli EA network represents a geographic/human network node — connecting Israeli AI safety organizing to the San Francisco-based labs and East Coast EA funding infrastructure. The reference to the Nevo brothers specifically highlights the personal relationship networks that underpin the structural funding relationships.
