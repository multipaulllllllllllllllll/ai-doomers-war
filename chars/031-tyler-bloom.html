Summary

Tyler Bloom is an AI safety commentator who has been tracking the partisan split in AI regulation positions - observing that Democrats have largely embraced the doomer framing while Republicans have positioned against it. His commentary covers the racing dynamics between Anthropic and OpenAI, Meta's internal safety debates, and the political economy of AI regulation.

Key Observations

Partisan Split on AI

Bloom observed that the AI regulation debate is crystallizing along partisan lines:

  • Democrats: Embrace doomer framing, support regulation, aligned with Obama/Sanders approach
  • Republicans: Skeptical of doomer narrative, anti-regulation, aligned with Trump position

    • This creates an unusual political dynamic where progressive anti-corporate rhetoric (Sanders) converges with establishment tech regulation (Obama Democrats) against the Republican free-market position.

    Meta's Position

    Bloom has also tracked Meta's internal debates about safety and whether to continue open-source model releases - a middle position between the closed-source incumbents (Anthropic/OpenAI) and the anti-regulation camp.
