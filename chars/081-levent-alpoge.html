Levent Alpöge

Author of a Lean proof of the Riemann Hypothesis

Involvement

The Lean FRO and Mathlib community reported that Alpöge released a Lean library on November 20. They distinguished compiling the code from verifying what it proved: two tools compared the theorem in the library with Mathlib’s independent statement of the Riemann Hypothesis. Their statement supports the proof attribution, not a laboratory affiliation. Lean FRO and Mathlib community.
