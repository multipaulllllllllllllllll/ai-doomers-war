Summary

Kevin Bass is an AI safety researcher and commentator who has been producing structural analysis of the doomer network - mapping how the same investors, labs, policy organizations, and political campaigns form a self-reinforcing system. His September 14, 2026 posts documented the self-funding loop connecting Jaan Tallinn, Anthropic, and ControlAI.

Key Structural Analysis

The Self-Funding Loop

Bass identified the core structural dynamic:

  • AI labs raise capital from doomer-aligned investors (Tallinn, Moskovitz, etc.)
  • Those investors fund policy organizations (ControlAI, MIRI, METR, etc.)
  • Policy orgs push for AI regulation framed as existential risk mitigation
  • Regulation creates competitive moats for established labs (Anthropic, OpenAI)
  • Moats allow labs to IPO at higher valuations
  • Investors' equity increases → more capital to deploy → cycle repeats

    • Tallinn Specific Loop

  • Tallinn invests in Anthropic
  • Tallinn funds ControlAI
  • ControlAI writes bills
  • Bills create regulatory moats for Anthropic
  • Anthropic IPO → Tallinn's equity increases
  • More funding for ControlAI

    • Significance

    Bass represents the structural critique of the doomer network - not a scientific disagreement about risk levels but a mapping of the financial and political incentives driving the regulatory campaign.
