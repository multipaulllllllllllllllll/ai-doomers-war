# Colette Kress

## Summary

Colette Kress is [[NVIDIA]]'s executive vice president and chief financial officer. She served as interim CEO from November 12 through Huang's reinstatement on November 23, 2026, retaining her EVP and CFO responsibilities throughout. The transition coincided with the frontier-AI lawsuit brought by Irreplaceable Education Fund, Public Citizen, Heat Initiative and Alliance for Secure AI. Reuters covered the suit but was not a plaintiff.

**Sources:** [NVIDIA transition](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition); [NVIDIA reinstatement](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director); [Plaintiffs announcement](https://www.citizen.org/news/coalition-of-nonprofits-files-landmark-lawsuit-to-stop-development-of-advanced-ai-systems-at-anthropic-google-meta-openai-and-spacexsi-that-proliferate-bioweapons-and-cyberattacks/).

## November 8-12, 2026: Investigation and interim appointment

On November 8, 2026, [[NVIDIA]]'s board initiated an independent investigation in light of reporting that Jensen Huang had been the target of a foreign intelligence effort. The board expressed confidence in his leadership and said it had no reason to believe national security or NVIDIA secrets had been compromised. That was the board's assessment, not a completed finding.

On November 10, the independent directors retained Joon H. Kim and Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen & Katz to conduct the investigation. Their statement did not express disappointment or demand Huang's resignation.

On November 12, NVIDIA announced Huang's immediate resignation as president, CEO and a director. [[Colette Kress]] became interim CEO while retaining her executive vice president and CFO posts; Harvey C. Jones became the board's first chair. The release did not announce an advisory role for Huang.

**Sources:** [November 8 board statement](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/statement-from-nvidia-board-of-directors); [November 10 counsel statement](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/statement-from-the-independent-members-of-the-nvidia-board); [November 12 transition](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition).

## November 17-23, 2026: Oversight, earnings and end of interim service

On November 17, NVIDIA established a National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee chaired by Suzanne Nora Johnson, with Stephen C. Neal and John O. Dabiri. The National Security Team and Global Trade Compliance Team report directly to that committee and to Kress for day-to-day operations. The committee does not report to Kress.

Kress led the November 17 Q3 earnings call. CBS reported her statement that licensed Hopper sales to China totaled $2.4 billion in the quarter, that NVIDIA was no longer shipping licensed Hopper data center products to China, and that guidance excluded those products. NVIDIA's earnings release excluded China data-center compute revenue from its $130 billion Q4 outlook. Past licensed sales, current shipment policy and license approvals are different measures.

On November 23, the board unanimously reinstated Huang as president, CEO and a director and thanked Kress for her interim service in addition to her continuing CFO responsibilities. Harvey Jones remained board chair. The independent investigation will proceed to conclusion; reinstatement was not its published finding.

## What to Watch

The independent investigation, ongoing government inquiries and California DOJ's November 19 subpoena remain unresolved in the retrieved record. NVIDIA announced a search for a Chief Compliance Officer reporting to the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee.
