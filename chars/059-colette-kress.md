# Colette Kress

## Summary

Colette Kress is NVIDIA's executive vice president and chief financial officer. On November 12, 2026, NVIDIA's board appointed her interim Chief Executive Officer after Jensen Huang resigned. She is the first NVIDIA CEO in the wiki's character graph; Huang remains a company-sheet figure without a person page. The appointment lands in the same week as Anthropic's public S-1 path, the Public Citizen / Reuters halt-frontier suit, and the White House Super Intelligence Force's existing-laws doctrine, which means the largest compute vendor in the race changed chief executives while the labs it finances were being sued to stop training.

## November 8-12, 2026: Investigation, request to resign, interim CEO

On November 8, 2026, NVIDIA's board of directors said it had initiated an independent investigation. This page records that company statement as the board's own predicate. It does not expand the investigation into a foreign-intelligence narrative that the recovered NVIDIA text does not carry.

On November 10, 2026, the independent members of the NVIDIA board said they were "deeply disappointed" in Jensen Huang and had asked him to resign. The same statement named Joon H. Kim of Wachtell, Lipton, Rosen and Katz as counsel.

On November 12, 2026, NVIDIA announced that Huang had resigned as CEO and that the board had appointed Kress "to serve as interim Chief Executive Officer, effective immediately." Huang "will remain with the company in an advisory capacity during the transition." Harvey C. Jones was elected board chair. Reuters the same day independently reported the resignation and described the board's stated predicate as personal misconduct / serious personal misconduct. Where Reuters later used "extramarital affairs," that wording is Reuters's, not NVIDIA's November 12 release, and this sheet keeps the two phrasings distinct.

Kress therefore inherits NVIDIA's dual posture from the September-October record: vendor of the Open Agent Safety Platform (OpenShell and Sentry, September 28) and equity investor across frontier labs, including the up-to-$10 billion Anthropic anchor discussed in earlier sweeps. The pope's September 28-29 critique of Huang (guardrails on the same afternoon as "no limits" and "no government regulation") now sits on a company whose named CEO is no longer Huang.
