# Alex Sobel

## Summary

Alex Sobel is a Labour Member of Parliament who introduced the UK's superintelligence ban bill — the first legislation of its kind in any G7 parliament — on September 8, 2026. The bill's language was drafted by ControlAI, the UK-based campaign non-profit funded by Jaan Tallinn and staffed by Conjecture alumni. Sobel served as the political vehicle for translating the ControlAI policy framework into parliamentary action.

## The UK Bill

Key provisions of the bill introduced September 8, 2026:
- Defines superintelligent AI as a system capable of "disempowering state authorities"
- Seeks state powers to monitor and restrict "precursor" systems
- Proposes a global treaty banning superintelligence development anywhere in the world

The Ten Minute Rule format means the bill received a first reading but is unlikely to become law without significant government backing. Its significance is political and symbolic — it establishes the legislative precedent and policy language for future action.
