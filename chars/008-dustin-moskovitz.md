# Dustin Moskovitz

## Summary

Dustin Moskovitz is the Facebook co-founder who, with his wife Cari Tuna, has deployed the largest private EA funding stream into AI safety organizations. His investment in Anthropic (leading the 2021 Series A) makes him simultaneously an investor in the lab and a funder of the organizations pushing for the regulation that would most benefit that lab. His funding of the Future of Life Institute — which coordinated the March 2023 AI pause letter signed by Elon Musk, Steve Wozniak, and Turing Award winners — is a documented example of his funding network functioning as a force multiplier for the doomer narrative.

## The Anthropic Investment

Moskovitz led Anthropic's Series A in 2021, investing directly in the lab that is now seeking a $2T IPO while advocating for the regulatory moats that make such a valuation defensible against open-source competition.

## Good Ventures / Coefficient Giving

Moskovitz and Tuna run Good Ventures (now evolving into Coefficient Giving). Their AI safety and biosecurity grant portfolio is the most significant private funding stream for the EA-aligned AI safety movement. Current documented grants (FY2025):

- **Founders Pledge**: $78.8M — funds AI safety organizations
- **FAR AI**: $14.4M
- **Effective Ventures USA**: $11.5M
- **Epoch AI**: receive funding from this network

## The Future of Life Institute

FLI is the organization that coordinated the March 2023 "Pause Giant AI Experiments" letter, which called for a 6-month pause on training runs beyond GPT-4 level. The letter was signed by Elon Musk, Steve Wozniak, and multiple Turing Award winners. It provided significant political cover for regulatory arguments and was cited in subsequent policy discussions. Moskovitz is documented as an early funder of FLI.

## The Guardian Story — Effort News Reporting

Brian Chau's Effort News (September 15, 2026) reported a significant finding: the same funding network — Dustin Moskovitz's Open Philanthropy (now Coefficient Giving) and the Open Society Foundations — sat behind the outlet, journalist, project, and experts in a single Guardian AI safety story. This represents a documented case of the funding network functioning as a media force multiplier.
