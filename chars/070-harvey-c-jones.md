# Harvey C. Jones

## Involvement

On November 12, [[019-nvidia|NVIDIA]] named Jones its first board chair as [[059-colette-kress|Colette Kress]] took over as interim CEO. Jones publicly backed Kress and the executive team. [NVIDIA transition, Nov 12](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition).

On November 23, Jones announced the board’s support for [[068-jensen-huang|Jensen Huang]]’s return. [[019-nvidia|NVIDIA]] retained Jones as chair, separating that position from the restored CEO role. [NVIDIA reinstatement, Nov 23](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director).
