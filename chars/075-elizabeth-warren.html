Elizabeth Warren

U.S. senator seeking records on NVIDIA exports

Involvement

On November 11, Warren and Chris Coons asked Commerce, State and the White House to preserve records on advanced NVIDIA chip exports and brief Congress. They sought written confirmation of preservation directives by November 17. Warren and Coons letter, Nov 11.

CNBC subsequently quoted Warren calling on Howard Lutnick and Jeffrey Kessler to explain why export-control loopholes had not been closed. CNBC, Nov 13.
