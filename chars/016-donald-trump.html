Summary

President Donald Trump characterized the AI doomer narrative as a "hoax and conspiracy" in a Truth Social post on September 14, 2026, framing the regulatory campaign as designed to slow American AI development to China's advantage. His position represents the Republican/conservative counter to the doomer regulatory agenda, skeptical of extinction-risk framing, supportive of rapid AI development, and hostile to the regulatory structures being proposed by Democrats and the doomer-aligned labs.

The "Hoax" Framing

Trump's argument: the doomer narrative is a cover for:

  • Regulatory capture by incumbent AI labs (Anthropic, OpenAI)
  • Slowing American AI development while China advances
  • Using fake existential risk claims to justify government intervention in private industry

    • @EchoIsMyID framing: "TRUMP V OBAMA on AI: The AI labs asked for a chaperone. Trump said no. Obama told Democrats to become one."

    Relationship to the Doomer Debate

    Trump's position aligns with:

  • Elon Musk (xAI), who is simultaneously calling AI dangerous while building xAI
  • Zuckerberg/Meta: reportedly aligned with the anti-regulatory camp
  • Open-source advocates who see the doomer narrative as a regulatory moat for closed-source incumbents

    • Sept 29-30: The Accord and the Renaming

    On September 29, 2026 Trump hosted ~20 tech titans at the White House (lunch in the Oval/Roosevelt; press in the East Room; North Lawn photo-op) and announced the answer to September's escape cascade would be self-policing, not statute: "The White House Accord on Superintelligence, Joint Commitment on Frontier SI Responsibilities," signed by six principals (Pichai, Amodei, Zuckerberg, Huang, Brockman, Musk). Trump called it "almost like a constitution" and "morally binding" ("there's going to be a tremendous self-policing aspect" (floated an oversight coordinator plus a 10-member advisory panel, and dismissed international guardrails: "I didn't want to do anything, because we're leading. Why would I want to do anything?"))

    That evening he signed an executive order formally renaming "artificial intelligence" to "super intelligence" in executive-branch communications: "It's not AI, it's SI. We've changed the name officially." California's Newsom administration responded by ordering state usage to keep "AI" (Sept 30), days after signing SB 53, so the terminology itself is now a federal-state fault line. Critics catalogued the accord as "no new legal requirements on any firm" (Ars Technica, via X receipts threads); the official document shipped with a typo (@HatsOffff).

    Sources: NBC, CNN, The Hill, sedaily, theunn, X (@HatsOffff); research/2026-09-30.md.

    Late September: UN "Hoax," AI Force, and the Pope's Answer

  • UN General Assembly (~Sept 22-26): Trump told the world body that AI-rogue fears are a "hoax"; announced he will launch an "AI Force" and an "AI czar" "to oversee" development, oversee-as-accelerate, against the rebrand EO (AI→"superintelligence" in official US documents, covered Sept 29-30). Axios: a White House official said the hoax framing targets Democrats "scoring points before the midterms."
  • Xi meeting in Washington (late Sept): Xi said AI must remain "always under human control"; Trump focused on the rebrand and dismissing regulation.
  • Contradicted by Pope Leo XIV by name (Sept 28, papal plane): concerns are "not fake news" and experts "should be taken seriously" (the first papal rebuttal of a US president's tech policy, three weeks before midterms. Trump now fights the doomer narrative on a moral front he can't out-rally: the pope.)

    • Sources: AP/PBS NewsHour, NewsNation, Thenewweb (Sept 28-29, 2026).
