# Rob Bonta

## Summary

Rob Bonta is California's Attorney General. California DOJ announced an investigative subpoena on [[NVIDIA]] on November 19, 2026. The agency is examining whether advanced chips or confidential information may have gone to China and whether NVIDIA misled investors or the public. The release frames questions for investigation, not established transfers or deception.

**Source:** [California DOJ, November 19](https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/attorney-general-bonta-serves-investigative-subpoena-nvidia).

## November 19, 2026: Investigative subpoena

California DOJ said information received through the subpoena would help it evaluate whether NVIDIA violated California law, following reporting of Huang's relationship with an alleged Chinese spy. The release announces an investigative demand, not an indictment or a finding that a law was violated. It is separate from federal export-control investigations and NVIDIA's independent board inquiry. Alleged intelligence links are not established by the subpoena itself.

Kress then held the interim CEO role while continuing as executive vice president and CFO. Huang's November 23 reinstatement did not resolve this state inquiry. NVIDIA said it would continue to cooperate with ongoing government investigations and inquiries.

**Sources:** [California DOJ, November 19](https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/attorney-general-bonta-serves-investigative-subpoena-nvidia); [NVIDIA transition](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition); [NVIDIA reinstatement](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director).

## What to Watch

Whether NVIDIA produces the requested information and whether California DOJ publishes findings or further action. No public resolution is established by the retrieved release; reinstatement is not regulatory clearance.
