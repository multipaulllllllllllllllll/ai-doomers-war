Vitalik Buterin

Co-founder, Ethereum

Summary

Vitalik Buterin is the co-founder of Ethereum who has become an important funder of AI existential risk organizations through Ethereum Foundation grants and personal giving. His funding bridges the cryptocurrency world to the Effective Altruism community - channeling resources from the crypto ecosystem into AI safety research organizations including MIRI and Epoch AI.

AI Safety Funding

Buterin's documented AI safety funding includes:

  • MIRI: Received Ethereum Foundation grants and personal donations through EA channels
  • Epoch AI: Funded through this pipeline

    • The Crypto-EA Bridge

    Buterin's role is as a bridging figure - bringing resources from the cryptocurrency ecosystem (which has generated enormous wealth) into the EA-aligned AI safety funding ecosystem. This represents a distinct funding pipeline separate from the Silicon Valley tech wealth of Tallinn/Moskovitz.

    Relationship to the Doomer Debate

    Buterin has expressed views on AI existential risk in public writing and Ethereum community discussions. His funding of MIRI places him in the same ecosystem as Tallinn and Moskovitz - supporting the research pipeline that trains researchers who later work at frontier labs and METR.
