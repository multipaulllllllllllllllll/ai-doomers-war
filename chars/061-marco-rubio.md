# Marco Rubio

## Summary

Marco Rubio is the U.S. Secretary of State in the second Trump administration. For this wiki he is a China-hawk node in the November 2026 [[NVIDIA]] export fight, not a Commerce officer and not the author of the Megaspeed memo. The New York Times (Nov. 12, 2026) is the primary placement: Rubio on the hawk side of [[NVIDIA]] chip sales to China in the same week Lutnick and Bessent blew up in Cabinet.

## November 12, 2026: NYT placement in the chip fight

The Times reported Rubio in the NVIDIA-China export argument on November 12, 2026, one day after Axios and AP described the Lutnick-Bessent Cabinet confrontation. This page treats that as a State Department alignment with the hawk faction ([[howard-lutnick]], House chairs [[brian-mast]], [[andrew-garbarino]], [[john-moolenaar]]), not as proof that Rubio wrote or signed the Megaspeed memo. A recovered full letter, if one exists, is not in the tenth-sweep cache and is not paraphrased here.

President [[donald-trump]] is the principal who can overrule both State and Commerce. Axios (Nov. 11, 2026) already had Trump saying he would "probably" overrule Lutnick. Rubio's public line after that "probably" was not independently recovered this sweep.
