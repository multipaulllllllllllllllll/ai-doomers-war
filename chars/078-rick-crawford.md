# Rick Crawford

## Involvement

On November 17, Crawford joined [[062-brian-mast|Brian Mast]] and [[064-john-moolenaar|John Moolenaar]] in announcing voluntary interviews with [[068-jensen-huang|Jensen Huang]] and [[019-nvidia|NVIDIA]] counsel [[071-tim-teter|Tim Teter]]. House Intelligence participated alongside Foreign Affairs and the China Select Committee. The chairs described national-security questions they wanted answered, not conclusions from the interviews. [House chairs’ joint statement, Nov 17](https://foreignaffairs.house.gov/news/press-releases/jensen-huang-and-nvidia-agree-to-appear-before-congress).
