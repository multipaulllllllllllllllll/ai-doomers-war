# Jim Banks

## Involvement

On November 12, Banks called for [[068-jensen-huang|Jensen Huang]] and [[060-howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]] to testify before Senate Banking. In the transcript released by his office, he linked the request to his AI Overwatch Act and argued that export rules had left loopholes. This was Banks’s criticism, not a committee finding. [Banks interview transcript, Nov 12](https://www.banks.senate.gov/news/press-releases/icymi-sen-banks-joins-america-reports-on-fox-news-channel-4/).
