# Jakub Pachocki

## Involvement

In a November 22 [[002-openai|OpenAI]] essay, Pachocki published internal pretraining measurements and proposed comparing developers’ progress using perplexity on held-out data. Lower perplexity means a model predicts the next token better on that data. He argued the measure could inform shared safety standards; the essay was a proposal, not an agreement among labs. [OpenAI, Nov 22](https://openai.com/index/perplexity-and-intelligence/).
