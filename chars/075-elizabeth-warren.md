# Elizabeth Warren

## Involvement

On November 11, Warren and Chris Coons asked Commerce, State and the White House to preserve records on advanced [[019-nvidia|NVIDIA]] chip exports and brief Congress. They sought written confirmation of preservation directives by November 17. [Warren and Coons letter, Nov 11](https://heisenbergreport.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/20261111_warren_coons_letter_to_wh_commerce_state_re_nvidia_record_retentionpdf.pdf).

CNBC subsequently quoted Warren calling on [[060-howard-lutnick|Howard Lutnick]] and [[072-jeffrey-kessler|Jeffrey Kessler]] to explain why export-control loopholes had not been closed. [CNBC, Nov 13](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/13/nvidia-scandal-lutnick-chip-sales.html).
