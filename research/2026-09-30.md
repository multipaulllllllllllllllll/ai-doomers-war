## 3. White House summit, the SI Accord, and the AI→SI renaming (Sept 29)

- NBC/CNN/The Hill: Tuesday Sept 29, Trump hosted ~20 tech titans for White House lunch (Amodei, Huang, Zuckerberg, Musk, Pichai, Bezos, Nadella, Su, Karp, Brockman; Vance, Bessent, Wiles, Johnson attending).
- "The White House Accord on Superintelligence, Joint Commitment on Frontier SI Responsibilities": voluntary commitments; four steps per the Truth Social post: (a) internal controls ensuring models meet standards on cybersecurity; (b) empowered internal team for controls/monitoring/detection; (c) external independent assessor of models; (d) independent board committee receiving audit reports and forcing remediation. Signatures on the document: Pichai, Amodei, Zuckerberg, Huang, Brockman, Musk (six + Trump).
- Trump: pledge "almost like a constitution," "morally binding," "tremendous self-policing aspect"; considering a committee to "watch over the whole entity"; administration plans an executive oversight coordinator + 10-member advisory panel (theunn summary); dismissed international treaty: "I didn't want to do anything, because we're leading. Why would I want to do anything?"
- Zuckerberg explained the accord: robust internal controls + detection, multiple layers of auditing (internal risk review, external auditors/evaluators), independent board committee review. "The idea isn't that this is the only thing we will ever do… it is a start and an accord the whole industry could come to."
- Executive order signed Tuesday evening formally renaming "artificial intelligence" → "super intelligence" in federal government communications. Trump: "It's not AI, it's SI. We've changed the name officially." (Applies to executive-branch communications; posts note Elon corrected a slip to SI mid-statement and Trump thanked him.)
