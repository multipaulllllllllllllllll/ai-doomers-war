# New sheet decisions: November 23, 2026

Section (d) was a lead list. The decisions below distinguish published actions, attributed reporting and scheduled appearances. Related entities are grouped only when the source describes a joint action.

## Add: Jensen Huang

On November 12, NVIDIA announced Huang’s resignation and named CFO Colette Kress interim CEO. Harvey C. Jones became the board’s first chair. [NVIDIA transition, Nov 12](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition).

On November 23, the board voted unanimously to restore Huang as president, CEO and director. It said it had spoken with Huang and received input from senior U.S. officials. Its assessment that his return posed no national security risk was the board’s conclusion, not a finding by investigators. [NVIDIA reinstatement, Nov 23](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director).

Huang agreed to a December 1 transcribed interview before three House committees. [House chairs’ joint statement, Nov 17](https://foreignaffairs.house.gov/news/press-releases/jensen-huang-and-nvidia-agree-to-appear-before-congress).

Senate Banking separately scheduled him as the witness at its December 3 public hearing on export controls. [Senate Banking hearing notice](https://www.banking.senate.gov/hearings/protecting-americas-ai-advantage-oversight-of-us-export-controls).

## Add: Alice Huang

Singapore’s Ministry of Home Affairs identified Huang Le, also known as Alice Huang, as Megaspeed’s then-CEO, chair and a key controller. She was present for a U.S. end-use check on June 24, 2025. Singapore police interviewed her that day in a separate investigation into server purchase declarations. [Singapore Ministry of Home Affairs](https://www.mha.gov.sg/media-room/newsroom/singapore-investigations-into-alice-huang-huang-le-and-megaspeed-international-pte-ltd/).

Police found insufficient evidence that she had committed a declaration offence. They warned her for giving inaccurate information about her role and barred her from re-entry. She left on February 26, 2026. The ministry explicitly said Singapore had not investigated her for espionage. [Singapore Ministry of Home Affairs](https://www.mha.gov.sg/media-room/newsroom/singapore-investigations-into-alice-huang-huang-le-and-megaspeed-international-pte-ltd/).

In November, Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton separately acknowledged an ongoing U.S. investigation into Huang and the company. That statement did not announce a charge. [CNBC, Nov 23](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/23/nvidia-megaspeed-chipmaker-us.html).

## Add: Harvey C. Jones

On November 12, NVIDIA named Jones its first board chair as Colette Kress took over as interim CEO. Jones publicly backed Kress and the executive team. [NVIDIA transition, Nov 12](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition).

[NVIDIA reinstatement, Nov 23](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-board-reinstates-jensen-huang-as-president-ceo-and-board-director).

## Add: Jeffrey Kessler

## Add: Tom Cotton

On October 13, 2025, Cotton wrote to Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick asking the department to investigate Megaspeed’s ownership, affiliations and ties to U.S. chip suppliers. His letter cited press reports; it records a request, not a determination that diversion occurred. ## Add: Jakub Pachocki

In a November 22 OpenAI essay, Pachocki published internal pretraining measurements and proposed comparing developers’ progress using perplexity on held-out data. Lower perplexity means a model predicts the next token better on that data. He argued the measure could inform shared safety standards; the essay was a proposal, not an agreement among labs. [OpenAI, Nov 22](https://openai.com/index/perplexity-and-intelligence/).

## Add: Levent Alpöge

The Lean FRO and Mathlib community reported that Alpöge released a Lean library on November 20. They distinguished compiling the code from verifying what it proved: two tools compared the theorem in the library with Mathlib’s independent statement of the Riemann Hypothesis. Their statement supports the proof attribution, not a laboratory affiliation. [Lean FRO and Mathlib community](https://leanprover-community.github.io/blog/posts/riemann-hypothesis/).

## Add: Megaspeed International

Commerce says it opened its investigation in 2024, requested NVIDIA sales records in May 2025 and conducted an end-use check in June, when NVIDIA put Megaspeed on its no-sell list. Justice subsequently took the criminal lead. The agencies describe suspected export-control violations, not a conviction. [Commerce and Justice joint statement](https://www.justice.gov/opa/pr/joint-statement-department-commerce-and-department-justice-megaspeed-investigation).

Singapore opened a separate investigation in February 2025 into possible inaccurate declarations for server purchases. Its ministry distinguished the ongoing company inquiry from the disposition of the case involving former CEO Alice Huang. [Singapore Ministry of Home Affairs](https://www.mha.gov.sg/media-room/newsroom/singapore-investigations-into-alice-huang-huang-le-and-megaspeed-international-pte-ltd/).

By October 2025, Megaspeed had also become the subject of a written request for investigation from Senator Tom Cotton. [Cotton letter, Oct 13, 2025](https://www.cotton.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/media/doc/101325lutnickletter.pdf).
